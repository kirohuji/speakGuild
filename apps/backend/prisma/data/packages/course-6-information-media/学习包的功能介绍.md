### Topic 01 · 识别主题

- **教学说明**：在"a transport notice"情境中学习识别主题。本Topic聚焦：Identifying the main topic quickly。
- **核心词（Vocabulary）**：`explanation`，`disruption`，`paragraphs`，`summarise`。

- **扩展词（Extension）**：`sentence`，`service`；补充词：`point`，`else`。

- **核心句块（Chunks）**：`The main point of this notice is that the schedule has changed.`；`If I had to summarise this in one sentence, it is about service disruption.`；`The key information is in the first two paragraphs.`；`Everything else is just background or explanation.`。
- **句型（Patterns）**：`The main point is that ___.`；`If I had to summarise: ___.`。
- **完成标准**：能在陌生变体中完成 12—16 轮互动，使用至少 3 个核心句块和 1 个主句型，并形成结论、决定或下一步。
- **口语输出**：围绕"a transport notice"完成 2—5 分钟未准备任务；不得照读对话。
- **微写作**：写 120—250 词，内容必须重新组织，不得抄写口语稿。
- **反馈与重做**：按任务完成、结构、词汇、语法及发音/拼写反馈；完成第二次重说和重写，7 天后换情境复测。

- **词汇扩展（Vocabulary Expansion）**：

  - `allegedly` (adv) — The document was allegedly leaked by a disgruntled insider with access to the files.

  - `invariably` (adv) — These heated discussions invariably seem to lead to the same frustrating conclusion.
  - `verification` (n) — Independent verification of the startling results will take at least several weeks.

# 基础③ · 日常工作与第三人称表达（L1）

## 学习包概览

本学习包是 **基础系列（Foundation）· 第 3 包**。学习者在包②已经能使用第一、二人称一般动词描述自己的生活，本包把表达视角自然转向工作中的其他人：介绍同事、说明岗位、描述日常职责、询问“他/她负责什么”，并在简单工作沟通中准确使用第三人称单数。

整个学习包使用一间小型公司或服务团队作为统一背景。学习者会反复接触经理、前台、设计师、助理、客服等常见角色，但教学重点不是背职业名称，而是学会自然说出：

- `She works in sales.`
- `He answers calls.`
- `She replies to emails.`
- `Does he work here?`
- `He doesn't handle this.`
- `She has the file.`

> 🎯 **核心目标**：学完本包，学习者能准确使用 `he / she / 单个人 + 动词-s/-es/-ies`，掌握 `does / doesn't / has`，并能围绕一位同事完成身份、地点、职责、工作安排和当前情况的基础交流。

> ✅ **结课能力**：独立完成一段同事介绍，询问并确认另一位同事的工作信息，并用 8—10 句制作一张完整、自然的“同事工作档案”。

### 为什么第三人称单数如此重要？

中文说“我工作、他工作、她回复邮件”时，动词本身不变化；英语中只要主语是 `he / she / it` 或一个具体的人，动词通常需要变化：

```text
I work.          → She works.
I go.            → He goes.
I study.         → She studies.
I have a file.   → He has a file.
```

中文母语者即使理解规则，也很容易在真实口语中漏掉 `-s`，或者在 `does / doesn't` 后继续错误地使用三单形式：

> ❌ 常见错误：*She work here. / *Does he works in sales? / *He doesn't answers calls.
> ✅ 正确表达：**She works here. / Does he work in sales? / He doesn't answer calls.**

本包通过同一工作场景中的大量人称转换、问答和纠错，让三单不再只是“知道规则”，而是形成稳定输出习惯。

### 和前两包的关系

| 学习包 | 主要视角 | 核心能力 | 代表表达 |
|:------|:---------|:---------|:---------|
| 基础① | I / you / it | 状态、身份与判断 | `I'm new here.` |
| 基础② | I / you / we | 自己的生活动作、喜好与需求 | `I work during the day.` |
| **基础③** | **he / she / 单个人** | **描述别人的工作与职责** | **`She works during the day.`** |

跨包复用的价值是人称转换，而不是重讲原来的生活话题：

```text
包②：I write messages.
包③：She writes emails.
包③：Does she write reports?
包③：She doesn't write reports.
```

### 适用对象

| 维度 | 说明 |
|:----|:-----|
| **难度** | L1（零基础—初级） |
| **前置要求** | 完成基础①和基础②，能使用 `I + 一般动词`、`Do you...?` |
| **适合人群** | 经常漏掉三单 `-s`，或不会使用 `does / doesn't / has` 的学习者 |
| **核心目标** | 在真实工作表达中形成稳定的第三人称输出习惯 |
| **学习方式** | 高频句块输入 → I/They 与 He/She 对比 → 问答与纠错 → 同事档案输出 |

### 覆盖核心语法

| 语法点 | 训练重点 | 高频表达示例 |
|:------|:---------|:-------------|
| 第三人称 be 动词 | `He/She is`，作为已学内容复用 | `She is our manager.` |
| 规则动词 `+s` | `works / helps / checks / starts` | `He checks the schedule.` |
| 结尾加 `-es` | `goes / does / watches / finishes` | `She finishes at five.` |
| 辅音 + y → `ies` | `studies / carries / replies / tries` | `He replies to emails.` |
| 特殊变化 | `have → has` | `She has the file.` |
| 第三人称否定 | `doesn't + 动词原形` | `He doesn't work here.` |
| 第三人称疑问 | `Does + 主语 + 动词原形` | `Does she handle calls?` |
| 特殊疑问句 | `What/Where/When/Who does...?` | `What does he do?` |
| 简短回答 | `Yes, he does. / No, she doesn't.` | 工作信息确认 |
| 所有格 | `his / her / 名词+'s` | `Her desk is over there.` |
| 宾格代词 | `him / her / them` | `Please ask her.` |
| 单复数主语对比 | `She works / They work` | 个人与团队 |
| 高频连接 | `and / but / so` | `She is busy, so please ask Ben.` |

## 设计哲学

### 🏢 统一工作微场景

本包不再使用“家人喜欢什么、他几点起床、她周末做什么”来重复包②，而是把全部三单训练放进一套连贯的日常工作微场景：

```text
第一天认识团队
→ 了解每个人在哪里工作
→ 了解每个人负责什么
→ 询问并确认工作信息
→ 描述个人与整个团队
```

同一批角色会跨 Topic 出现，使学习者既保持情境连续，也能在变化的沟通任务中重复三单结构。

### 🗣️ 高频工作表达优先

本包选择可迁移到办公室、门店、学校、酒店、诊所和服务团队的基础表达：

- `This is my colleague, Mia.`
- `She works at the front desk.`
- `He helps customers.`
- `Who handles this?`
- `Please ask her.`
- `She is busy right now.`
- `He has the file.`
- `They work together.`

不使用过度专业的项目管理或商务术语，也不为了展示语法加入低频、难输出的词。

### 🧩 两层词汇分配策略

| 层级 | 定义 | 使用位置 |
|:----|:-----|:---------|
| **核心词（Core）** | 与当前工作任务直接相关、必须主动掌握的词 | 必须进入核心句块和句型示例 |
| **扩展词（Extension）** | 能进入同一句型，帮助练习三单变化的词 | 必须进入 `warmup_pipeline` |

例如在“日常职责”中，`answer, call, write, email, help, customer` 是核心词；`check, print, copy, send` 可以作为扩展词进入三单替换练习。

### 🔗 累加式设计（Cumulative Design）

```text
场景 A 认识同事和团队
→ 建立 he / she / his / her 与岗位、地点表达

场景 B 工作职责和安排
→ 集中训练 -s / -es / -ies

场景 C 询问与确认
→ 集中训练 Does / doesn't / has

场景 D 个人与团队协作
→ 对比单复数，并完成综合同事档案
```

语法投放严格按 Topic 顺序执行：

```text
Topic 01—08：只练第三人称肯定表达，不提前使用 Does / doesn't / has
Topic 09：引入 Does 与简短回答
Topic 10：在 Does 基础上加入 What / Where / When / Who
Topic 11：引入 doesn't + 动词原形
Topic 12：引入 has / Does ... have
Topic 13—16：复用前面结构完成团队协作与综合输出
```

### 📐 人称转换驱动

包②的已学一般动词在本包不重新解释词义，而是直接用于三单转换：

| 已学表达 | 本包转换 | 进一步问答 |
|:---------|:---------|:-----------|
| `I work here.` | `She works here.` | `Does she work here?` |
| `I write emails.` | `He writes emails.` | `What does he write?` |
| `I have a meeting.` | `She has a meeting.` | `Does she have a meeting?` |
| `They start at nine.` | `He starts at nine.` | `When does he start?` |

## 逐话题教学说明与对话 List

本节对应 `training_topics.csv` 的教学说明，并定义一组贯穿全包的固定人物。后续剧集可以沿用相同人物关系，使三单训练发生在连续、可理解的工作环境中。

### 固定人物

| 人物 | 身份 | 主要用途 |
|:----|:-----|:---------|
| **Lin** | 新加入团队的员工，学习者主要代入角色 | 提问、确认和介绍同事 |
| **Mia** | 设计师，负责邮件和客户资料 | `she / her / replies / writes` |
| **Ben** | 前台助理，负责电话和访客 | `he / him / answers / helps` |
| **Emma** | 团队经理 | `manager / has / handles / asks` |

### 🅰️ 认识同事与团队

#### Topic 01 · 同事与岗位

- **教学说明**：学习用 `he / she` 介绍同事及其岗位，建立第三人称视角。
- **学习重点**：`He/She is our...`、`名字 + is a/an...`，不再把包①的 `This is my...` 登记为新句型。
- **完成标准**：能用不同主语介绍至少四种基础岗位。
- **对话场景**：Lin 入职第一天，经理带他认识团队。
- **人物**：新员工 Lin；经理 Emma；同事 Mia、Ben。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Emma: Lin, Mia is our designer.
  2. Lin: She is the designer for this team.
  3. Emma: Right. She is also your new colleague.
  4. Mia: Hi, Lin. Welcome to the team.
  5. Emma: Ben is our receptionist.
  6. Lin: Is he also an assistant?
  7. Emma: Yes. He is a front desk assistant.
  8. Lin: What about Alex?
  9. Emma: He is our engineer.
  10. Lin: Great. Emma is the manager, Mia is the designer, Ben is the receptionist, and Alex is the engineer.

#### Topic 02 · 部门与地点

- **教学说明**：学习用肯定句说明同事所属部门、工作位置和公司。
- **学习重点**：`works in / works at / works for` 三种常见搭配。
- **完成标准**：能根据团队图说出三个人的工作部门或位置。
- **对话场景**：Emma 带 Lin 参观办公室。
- **人物**：Lin；经理 Emma；前台助理 Ben。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Emma: There are three main departments in our company.
  2. Lin: This area is the sales department.
  3. Emma: Right. Sofia works in sales.
  4. Lin: The design department is next to sales.
  5. Emma: Mia works in design.
  6. Lin: Ben is at the front desk.
  7. Emma: Yes. He works in customer support.
  8. Lin: The support department is near the entrance.
  9. Emma: Exactly. Everyone works for the same company.
  10. Lin: Now I can find sales, design, support, and the front desk.

#### Topic 03 · 介绍一位同事

- **教学说明**：学习用 `his / her / our` 说明同事的角色、背景和简单特点。
- **学习重点**：`His/Her role is...`、`His/Her background is...`、`Our colleague is...`。
- **完成标准**：能根据人物卡片完整介绍一位同事。
- **对话场景**：Lin 向新访客介绍 Mia。
- **人物**：Lin；访客 Sofia；设计师 Mia。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Lin: Mia is our colleague in design.
  2. Sofia: What is her role?
  3. Lin: Her role is product designer.
  4. Sofia: What is her background?
  5. Lin: Her background is in visual design.
  6. Sofia: She seems friendly.
  7. Lin: Yes. She is a friendly colleague.
  8. Mia: Hi, Sofia. Welcome.
  9. Sofia: Thanks. Lin told me about your role and Ben's role.
  10. Mia: Yes. His role is customer support, and my background is in design.

#### Topic 04 · 联系信息

- **教学说明**：学习说明同事的分机、工位和内部通讯录信息，并用完整句块指引联系。
- **学习重点**：`You can reach...`、`His/Her extension is...`、`check the directory`。
- **完成标准**：能告诉别人如何找到或联系某位同事。
- **对话场景**：访客需要联系 Ben。
- **人物**：访客 Lin；经理 Emma；前台助理 Ben。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Lin: I need Ben's contact details.
  2. Emma: You can reach him at the front desk.
  3. Lin: Which desk is his?
  4. Emma: His desk is beside the entrance.
  5. Lin: What is his extension?
  6. Emma: His extension is 204.
  7. Lin: Is it in the staff directory?
  8. Emma: Yes. The directory lists his contact details.
  9. Lin: Great. I can reach him at extension 204.
  10. Emma: Right, or you can visit his desk.

### 🅱️ 工作职责与安排

#### Topic 05 · 开始一天

- **教学说明**：学习用肯定句描述同事开始工作时依次完成的基础动作。
- **学习重点**：`starts / checks / opens / logs in` 的第三人称形式。
- **完成标准**：能用三个三单动作描述一位同事的早晨工作流程。
- **对话场景**：Emma 向 Lin 说明 Mia 的开工流程。
- **人物**：新员工 Lin；经理 Emma。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Emma: Mia starts work at nine.
  2. Lin: She starts with her calendar.
  3. Emma: Right. She checks her schedule first.
  4. Lin: Then she opens the design system.
  5. Emma: Yes, and she logs in to the team account.
  6. Lin: Her calendar shows the first task.
  7. Emma: She checks that task before the team meeting.
  8. Lin: So she starts, checks, opens, and logs in.
  9. Emma: Exactly. That is her start-of-day routine.
  10. Lin: I can describe how she starts work now.

#### Topic 06 · 电话与邮件

- **教学说明**：学习用第三人称肯定句描述接听、书写、转发、添加附件、打印等通讯职责。
- **学习重点**：`answers / writes / forwards / attaches / prints`。
- **完成标准**：能准确说出 Ben 和 Mia 分别处理什么。
- **对话场景**：Lin 向经理确认团队分工。
- **人物**：Lin；经理 Emma。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Emma: Ben answers the main line.
  2. Lin: He answers customer calls.
  3. Emma: Mia writes the daily email.
  4. Lin: She writes the team report too.
  5. Emma: Right. She forwards the report to me.
  6. Lin: She attaches the final document.
  7. Emma: Ben prints one copy for the front desk.
  8. Lin: So Ben answers and prints.
  9. Emma: And Mia writes, forwards, and attaches the report.
  10. Lin: Their communication duties are clear.

#### Topic 07 · 帮助客户

- **教学说明**：学习描述服务人员如何接待客户、解释问题、提供方案并引导下一步。
- **学习重点**：`guides / explains / solves` 及动作后的宾语。
- **完成标准**：能用三句说明一位服务人员的基础职责。
- **对话场景**：Lin 观察 Ben 接待一位访客。
- **人物**：Lin；前台助理 Ben；访客 Sofia。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Sofia: Hi, I have a question.
  2. Ben: Welcome. Please tell me the question.
  3. Lin: Ben guides each customer at the front desk.
  4. Ben: First, I explain the next step.
  5. Sofia: I need a solution for this visitor request.
  6. Ben: This simple solution should work.
  7. Lin: Ben solves basic problems here.
  8. Sofia: He explains the solution clearly.
  9. Ben: Then I guide the customer to the right department.
  10. Lin: Ben guides, explains, and solves customer problems.

#### Topic 08 · 完成与跟进

- **教学说明**：学习描述同事完成任务、赶在截止时间前处理工作并进行后续跟进。
- **学习重点**：`finishes`、`tries`、`carries` 以及 `follows up`。
- **完成标准**：能根据工作清单正确变化四个三单动词。
- **对话场景**：下班前，Emma 确认 Mia 是否完成客户跟进。
- **人物**：经理 Emma；设计师 Mia；Lin。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Emma: Mia finishes the report before the deadline.
  2. Lin: She tries to complete it by four.
  3. Emma: She follows up with the customer after that.
  4. Lin: Her follow-up completes the task.
  5. Emma: Ben carries the printed copies downstairs.
  6. Lin: He carries them before five.
  7. Emma: Mia finishes her final check at five.
  8. Lin: She tries to finish every task on time.
  9. Emma: Right. She completes the report and follows up.
  10. Lin: And Ben carries the copies to the front desk.

### 🅲 询问、否定与确认

#### Topic 09 · Does 问答

- **教学说明**：学习用 `Does...?` 询问第三人称工作信息，并作肯定或否定简短回答。
- **学习重点**：`Does` 后的实义动词必须还原为原形。
- **完成标准**：能把三单肯定句转换成 Does 问句并正确回答。
- **对话场景**：Lin 核对本周值班表。
- **人物**：Lin；经理 Emma。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Lin: I need to confirm Ben's shift.
  2. Lin: Does Ben start at eight?
  3. Emma: No, he doesn't. He starts at nine.
  4. Lin: Does he answer the main line?
  5. Emma: Yes, he does. That is one duty on his shift.
  6. Lin: Does Mia print the report?
  7. Emma: No, she doesn't. Ben prints it.
  8. Lin: Is that information correct?
  9. Emma: Yes. This confirms each person's duty.
  10. Lin: Good. I can confirm the shift now.

#### Topic 10 · 询问具体信息

- **教学说明**：学习使用 `What / Where / When / Who does...?` 获取任务、地点、时间和人员信息。
- **学习重点**：疑问词后使用 `does + 动词原形`，回答恢复第三人称形式。
- **完成标准**：能围绕同一位同事提出四种不同信息问题。
- **对话场景**：Lin 填写团队工作信息表。
- **人物**：Lin；经理 Emma。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Lin: What task does Mia complete first?
  2. Emma: She checks the daily schedule.
  3. Lin: Where does she work?
  4. Emma: Her work location is the design department.
  5. Lin: When does she finish the report?
  6. Emma: She finishes it at four.
  7. Lin: Who does she contact after that?
  8. Emma: She contacts the customer.
  9. Lin: I have every detail: task, location, time, and person.
  10. Emma: Good. The information sheet is complete.

#### Topic 11 · 否定与纠正

- **教学说明**：学习使用 `doesn't + 动词原形` 否定错误信息，再补充准确事实。
- **学习重点**：`doesn't work` 不能说成 `doesn't works`；用 `actually / instead` 完成自然纠正。
- **完成标准**：能完成三组“错误信息—否定—准确事实”。
- **对话场景**：Lin 的工作记录有错误，Emma 帮他修改。
- **人物**：Lin；经理 Emma。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Lin: My first note says Mia answers the main line.
  2. Emma: That is a mistake.
  3. Emma: She doesn't answer it.
  4. Lin: What is the accurate fact?
  5. Emma: She actually writes the daily report instead.
  6. Lin: My second note says Ben works in design.
  7. Emma: He doesn't work there.
  8. Emma: He actually works at the front desk instead.
  9. Lin: Thanks. These corrections make the facts accurate.
  10. Emma: Good. Always use the verb's base form after `doesn't`.

#### Topic 12 · have 与 has

- **教学说明**：学习用 `has` 表达某人持有会议安排、文件或设备，并在 Does 问句中还原为 `have`。
- **学习重点**：`She has...`、`Does she have...?`，不能说 `Does she has...?`。
- **完成标准**：能正确完成 `I have / She has / Does she have` 三种转换。
- **对话场景**：会议开始前寻找材料和设备。
- **人物**：Lin；经理 Emma；设计师 Mia。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Lin: Mia has a meeting at ten.
  2. Emma: Right. She has the main file.
  3. Lin: Does she have the meeting document too?
  4. Emma: Yes, she does.
  5. Lin: Ben has another device.
  6. Emma: He has the presentation equipment.
  7. Lin: Does he have the final document?
  8. Emma: No, he doesn't. Mia has it.
  9. Lin: So Mia has the file and document, and Ben has the equipment.
  10. Emma: Correct. Everything is ready for the meeting.

### 🅳 个人与团队协作

#### Topic 13 · 一个人与一组人

- **教学说明**：学习对比单个人、两个人和一组人作主语时的动词形式。
- **学习重点**：`She works / They work`、`He has / They have`。
- **完成标准**：看到单人或多人信息时，能选择正确动词形式。
- **对话场景**：Lin 向访客介绍个人与团队分工。
- **人物**：Lin；访客 Sofia；团队成员 Mia 和 Ben。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Lin: Mia is one individual in the design group.
  2. Lin: She writes the daily report.
  3. Sofia: Ben is another member of the staff.
  4. Lin: Right. He answers the main line.
  5. Sofia: Several staff members support customers.
  6. Lin: Yes. They work as a group.
  7. Sofia: The two members have different duties.
  8. Lin: One individual writes, and the other answers.
  9. Sofia: Several staff members support the same company.
  10. Lin: Exactly. Their jobs are different, but every role is important.

#### Topic 14 · 谁负责

- **教学说明**：学习询问谁负责某项工作，并用宾格代词完成指派和转交。
- **学习重点**：`Who handles...?`、`be responsible for...`、`assign/transfer ... to him/her/them`。
- **完成标准**：能把一项工作准确转交给合适的个人或团队。
- **对话场景**：访客带来一份需要处理的材料。
- **人物**：访客 Sofia；Lin；经理 Emma。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Sofia: Who handles this document?
  2. Lin: Mia handles it.
  3. Emma: She is responsible for customer documents.
  4. Sofia: Can I transfer this task to her?
  5. Emma: Yes. I can assign it to Mia.
  6. Sofia: Who handles the printed copies?
  7. Emma: Ben and Alex handle them.
  8. Sofia: Can I transfer the copies to them?
  9. Lin: Yes. Emma assigns one part to him and one part to Alex.
  10. Sofia: Great. Each task now has a responsible person.

#### Topic 15 · 当前工作状态

- **教学说明**：学习说明同事是否可联系、暂时被占用、事情是否紧急以及何时返回。
- **学习重点**：`available / occupied / urgent / return`，旧的 `busy/free/need` 只作前置复用。
- **完成标准**：能根据同事状态决定等待、转交或之后再联系。
- **对话场景**：Lin 想找 Mia，但她暂时无法回应。
- **人物**：Lin；前台助理 Ben；设计师 Mia。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Lin: Is Mia available now?
  2. Ben: No. She is occupied with a meeting.
  3. Lin: Is my request urgent?
  4. Ben: No, so a short delay is okay.
  5. Lin: When does she return?
  6. Ben: She returns at three.
  7. Lin: I can wait until then.
  8. Ben: Good. She knows you are waiting.
  9. Lin: Please tell her the request is not urgent.
  10. Ben: I will. She will contact you when she is available.

#### Topic 16 · 同事工作档案

- **教学说明**：综合岗位、地点、职责、问答、否定、has 和团队对比，组织一份完整的同事工作档案。
- **学习重点**：使用 `introduce / describe / main / overall / summary / profile` 组织信息，而不是机械罗列句型。
- **完成标准**：不看完整范文，用8—10句介绍一位同事，并回答两个追问。
- **对话场景**：Lin 向新员工介绍 Mia 的工作档案。
- **人物**：Lin；新员工 Alex；设计师 Mia。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Lin: Let me introduce Mia.
  2. Lin: Her profile says she is our designer.
  3. Alex: Can you describe her main role?
  4. Lin: Her main role is preparing customer designs.
  5. Alex: Where does she work?
  6. Lin: She works in the design department.
  7. Alex: What does she complete each day?
  8. Lin: She writes a report and follows up with customers.
  9. Alex: Does she answer the main line?
  10. Lin: No, she doesn't. Ben answers it.
  11. Alex: What is your overall summary?
  12. Lin: In summary, Mia is an organized designer who supports the whole team.

## 附：第三人称单数动词变化速查卡

```text
一般情况加 -s：
work → works
help → helps
start → starts
check → checks

s / sh / ch / x / o 结尾加 -es：
finish → finishes
watch → watches
go → goes
do → does

辅音 + y：去 y 加 -ies：
reply → replies
carry → carries
try → tries
study → studies

特殊变化：
have → has

does / doesn't 后回到原形：
Does she work here?
She doesn't work here.
Does he have the file?
```

## 附：人称代词与所有格速查卡

```text
主语        所有格       宾语

she         her          her

名词所有格：

```

### 双输出通过规则

```text
□ 口语和微写作都完成真实任务，而不是只展示目标句
□ 首次输出允许错误，但必须根据反馈完成第二版
□ 第二版需修正至少一个影响理解、连贯或得体度的问题
□ 延迟复习更换人物、时间、地点或原因，检查能否迁移
```

## 设计检查清单

```text
□ 每个 Topic 对应真实、常见的基础工作沟通任务
□ 全包使用统一工作微场景，角色与情境前后连贯
□ 不重复包②的家人、起床、吃饭、周末和天气话题
□ Topic 01—08 的正式教学和对话不提前使用 Does / doesn't / has
□ 16 个 Topic 各有 4 个独立核心句块和 2 个独立句型
□ 每个核心词至少进入一个核心句块和一个句型示例
□ 每个扩展词都进入 warmup_pipeline
□ 包①、包②已登记词只作前置复用，不再计入本包词表
□ -s、-es、-ies、has 均有集中且充分的输出训练
□ Does / doesn't 后的动作词全部使用原形
□ 工作词汇保持基础、通用，不扩张到复杂商务英语
□ Topic 16 不增加新语法，只增加组织同事档案所需的表达词
```

### 常见设计错误

| 错误类型 | 问题 | 调整原则 |
|:---------|:-----|:---------|
| 用家庭日常重复包② | 用户只感到主语变了 | 用工作职责形成新的交际任务 |
| 只背三单拼写表 | 知道规则但说话仍漏 `-s` | 在高频句块、人称转换和问答中反复输出 |
| Does 后继续加 `-s` | 助动词与实义动词重复变化 | 专门设置纠错训练 |
| 核心词难度过高 | L1 学习者无法主动使用 | 保持常见岗位、动作和基础状态词 |
| 叙事和时态过早混入 | 学习主线分散 | 本包稳定一般现在时第三人称 |

### 跨包词汇复用速查

| 复用内容 | 来源 | 本包使用方式 |
|:---------|:-----|:-------------|
| be 动词、姓名、位置 | 基础① | `She is... / Her desk is...` |
| 日常一般动词 | 基础② | 转换为 `He/She + 动词-s` |
| 时间表达 | 基础② | `She starts at nine.` |
| need / have | 基础② | `He needs... / She has...` |
| 电话、消息、约见词 | 基础② | 转换为同事工作沟通 |

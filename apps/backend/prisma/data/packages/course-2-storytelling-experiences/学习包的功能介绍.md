# Course ② · 叙事与经历讲述（L3）

> 从“我做了什么”升级为有背景、过程、转折、感受和结果的完整故事。

## 学习包概览

| 维度 | 设计 |
|:-----|:-----|
| CEFR | B1 |
| 前置要求 | Course ①；一般过去时和完成时基础 |

```text
建立背景 → 事件开始 → 过程推进 → 意外转折 → 结果与反思
```
### 本包新语法

> Foundation 已封版。以下中级语法通过本包的交际场景首次系统引入。

| 语法点 | 本包中的场景应用 | 核心句式 |
|:-------|:----------------|:---------|
| **过去进行时** | 讲述"当时正在发生什么"，交代故事背景 | `I **was reading** when you called.` / `While I **was walking**, I saw her.` |

### Topic 01 · 时间地点

- **教学说明**：在"a first day at a new school"情境中学习时间地点。本Topic聚焦：Setting the scene with time, place, and initial atmosphere。
- **核心词（Vocabulary）**：`background`,`event`,`sequence`,`turning point`。
- **扩展词（Extension）**：`eventually`,`fortunately`。
- **核心句块（Chunks）**：`It all started on a rainy Monday morning in September.`；`The place was unfamiliar, and I did not know a single person there.`；`I remember the exact moment I walked through the front door.`；`Looking back, that ordinary morning turned out to be anything but ordinary.`。
- **句型（Patterns）**：`It all started when ___.`；`I remember the exact moment when ___.`。
- **完成标准**：能在陌生变体中完成 12—16 轮互动，使用至少 3 个核心句块和 1 个主句型，并形成结论、决定或下一步。
- **口语输出**：围绕"a first day at a new school"完成 2—5 分钟未准备任务；不得照读对话。
- **微写作**：写 120—250 词，内容必须重新组织，不得抄写口语稿。
- **反馈与重做**：按任务完成、结构、词汇、语法及发音/拼写反馈；完成第二次重说和重写，7 天后换情境复测。
- **跨包复习**：Foundation 6、8、10 的过去时、连接和从句；本 Topic 新表达须在本包后续至少两个 Topic 再次主动调用。
- **具体对话**：
  1. Ben: Can you tell me about a first day at a new school?
  2. Lin: Sure. It all started on a rainy Monday morning in September. I was fourteen.
  3. Ben: What was the first thing you noticed?
  4. Lin: The place was unfamiliar, and I did not know a single person there. Everyone seemed to already have friends.
  5. Ben: That sounds lonely.
  6. Lin: It was. I remember the exact moment I walked through the front door. The noise of the hallway hit me first.
  7. Ben: How did the day turn out?
  8. Lin: Looking back, that ordinary morning turned out to be anything but ordinary. By lunchtime, I had met my best friend.

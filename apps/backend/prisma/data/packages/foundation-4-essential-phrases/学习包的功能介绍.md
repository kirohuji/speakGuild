# 基础④ · 高频日常动词短语 · 动词 + 小品词（L1~L2）

## 学习包概览

本学习包是 **基础系列（Foundation）· 第4包**。前三包已经让学习者掌握基础状态、一般动作和第三人称表达；本包进一步解决日常口语中的高频难点：同一个基础动词与不同小品词或介词组合后，会形成新的完整意义。

学习者不再只认识 `turn / put / look / get`，而是把下面这些表达作为完整语言单位理解和使用：

- `turn on / turn off`
- `put on / put away`
- `look at / look for / look after`
- `get in / get out / get back`
- `pick up / put down`

> 🎯 **核心目标**：掌握80个新增核心动词短语和32个Warmup扩展短语，理解 `up / down / in / out / on / off / back / over / away` 的基础语义，并能在口语中正确处理普通名词和代词宾语的位置。

> ✅ **结课能力**：能听懂并主动使用至少80个高频日常动词短语；能判断基础短语是否可分；能自然说出 `Turn it on. / Pick it up. / Look for him.` 等正确表达，并在连续对话中完成起居、物品处理、出行、沟通、学习和社交任务。

### 为什么动词短语需要一个专门学习包？

英语口语大量依赖“基础动词 + 小品词”：

```text
turn       → turn on / turn off / turn down
put        → put on / put down / put away / put back
get        → get in / get out / get on / get off / get back
look       → look at / look for / look after / look around
```

学习者即使认识每个单词，也不一定理解组合后的整体意思。例如：

```text
find       = 找到
find out   = 查明、弄清楚

work       = 工作
work out   = 解决（问题）

show       = 展示
show up    = 出现、到场
```

因此，本包不把短语拆成孤立单词记忆，而是通过“完整句块 → 宾语位置 → 句型替换 → 连续对话”建立整体反应。

## 设计哲学

### 🧩 完整短语是最小教学单位

`pick up` 是一个核心词条，不拆成 `pick` 和 `up` 两个独立词。跨包和包内查重也以完整短语为单位：

```text
pick up ≠ put up ≠ wake up
```

同一个小品词可以进入多个短语，但同一个完整短语只能登记一次。

### 📐 每个核心短语必须有两个句块

两个句块分别承担：

1. 最常见的完整场景表达；
2. 代词位置、自然回应或另一种基础搭配。

例如：

```text
Please turn on the light.
The room is dark. Turn it on.
```

### 🔧 句型固定短语，替换宾语或情境

正确：

```text
Pick up ___.
Look through ___ before ___.
___ shows up at ___.
```

不采用：

```text
___ up the bag.
```

动词和小品词构成固定意义，不能让学习者随意猜配。

### 🏗️ 三类结构必须明确区分

| 类型 | 示例 | 宾语规则 |
|:----|:-----|:---------|
| 不带宾语 | `sit down / come in / show up` | 直接使用完整短语 |
| 可分短语 | `turn on / pick up / put away` | 名词可放中间或后面；代词必须放中间 |
| 不可分短语 | `look for / look after / look through` | 宾语必须放在介词后面 |

### 📊 核心与扩展短语分工

| 层级 | 数量 | 使用要求 |
|:----|:----:|:---------|
| 核心短语 | 每Topic 5个 | 必须进入2个句块、1个句型和所属对话 |
| 扩展短语 | 每Topic 2个 | 必须进入Warmup的辨义、选词、构句和情境回应 |

### 🔗 与L2专项动词短语课程的边界

包④只教授80个新增核心短语的一个高频含义。`course-phrasal-verbs-daily` 后续扩展到200个L2短语、多义用法和更复杂时态，因此两者是“基础入口 → 专项扩展”的关系。

## 逐话题教学说明与对话 List

本节定义一组贯穿全包的固定人物，后续剧集沿用相同人物关系，使短语训练发生在连续、可理解的日常环境中。

### 固定人物

| 人物 | 身份 | 对话作用 |
|:----|:-----|:---------|
| **Lin** | 学习者主要代入角色 | 执行动作、提问和复述短语 |
| **Mia** | Lin的朋友 | 居家、物品、社交场景 |
| **Ben** | 同事 | 工作、设备和任务场景 |
| **Emma** | 课程老师 | 解释短语、纠正宾语位置 |

### 🅰️ 动作、状态与整理

#### Topic 01 · 起身与开口

- **教学说明**：学习用 `up` 表达向上、开始和提高声音。
- **核心短语**：`wake up, stand up, sit up, hurry up, speak up`
- **结构重点**：这5个短语在本话题按不带宾语的完整动作使用。
- **完成标准**：能听从并发出起床、起身、坐直、加快和大声说话的指令。
- **场景与人物**：早晨准备出门；Emma提醒Lin。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Emma: Lin, the alarm is loud. Wake up.
  2. Lin: I wake up at seven every day.
  3. Emma: It is seven now. Please stand up.
  4. Lin: All right. I am up.
  5. Emma: Sit up straight first.
  6. Lin: Okay. My back is straight.
  7. Emma: We start at eight. Hurry up.
  8. Lin: Sorry. My voice is quiet in the morning.
  9. Emma: Then speak up, please.
  10. Lin: Good morning! I am ready now.

#### Topic 02 · 整理与完成

- **教学说明**：学习用 `up` 表达整理干净、全部用完或改善情绪。
- **核心短语**：`clean up, tidy up, use up, eat up, cheer up`
- **结构重点**：练习可分短语中的名词和代词位置。
- **完成标准**：能正确说出 `Clean it up. / Use it up. / Cheer her up.`。
- **场景与人物**：早餐后整理厨房；Lin和Mia。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Mia: The kitchen is messy. Please clean up the table.
  2. Lin: Okay. I clean it up after breakfast.
  3. Mia: Please tidy up the chairs too.
  4. Lin: I put them in the right place.
  5. Mia: Do not use up all the water.
  6. Lin: No problem. I only use a little.
  7. Mia: Your breakfast is still here. Eat it up.
  8. Lin: I am not very hungry.
  9. Mia: Cheer up. The food is good.
  10. Lin: You are right. I eat it up and finish the cleaning.

#### Topic 03 · 坐下与放慢

- **教学说明**：学习用 `down` 表达向下、降低速度、平静和记录。
- **核心短语**：`sit down, lie down, slow down, calm down, write down`
- **结构重点**：区分不带宾语的动作和可分的 `write down`。
- **完成标准**：能在休息和课堂场景中正确使用5个短语。
- **场景与人物**：Lin身体不舒服，在Emma的教室休息。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Emma: You look tired. Please sit down.
  2. Lin: Thanks. I sit down by the window.
  3. Emma: Lie down on the sofa and rest.
  4. Lin: The sofa is comfortable.
  5. Emma: Your breathing is fast. Slow down.
  6. Lin: Okay. I breathe slowly.
  7. Emma: Calm down. Everything is okay.
  8. Lin: Thank you. What is the next class task?
  9. Emma: Write down these three words.
  10. Lin: I write them down in my notebook.

#### Topic 04 · 降低与停止

- **教学说明**：继续学习 `down`，表达放下、调低、关闭、冷却和停止运转。
- **核心短语**：`put down, turn down, shut down, cool down, break down`
- **结构重点**：前三个为可分结构；`cool down / break down` 在本话题按不带宾语的状态变化使用。
- **完成标准**：能正确处理 `Put it down. / Turn it down. / Shut it down.`。
- **场景与人物**：办公室设备声音过大并出现问题；Ben和Lin。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Ben: That box is heavy. Put it down.
  2. Lin: I put the box down by the desk.
  3. Ben: The music is loud. Please turn it down.
  4. Lin: The volume is low now.
  5. Ben: The computer is hot. Shut it down.
  6. Lin: I shut the computer down.
  7. Ben: It needs time to cool down.
  8. Lin: The printer is also very slow.
  9. Ben: Old printers often break down.
  10. Lin: The computer is off, and the printer is quiet now.

### 🅱️ 进出、开关与移动

#### Topic 05 · 进入与加入

- **教学说明**：学习用 `in` 表达进入空间、进入车辆、填写和参加。
- **核心短语**：`come in, go in, get in, fill in, join in`
- **结构重点**：`fill in` 可分；`join in` 后接活动时不拆开。
- **完成标准**：能在入口和报名场景中完成进入、填写和参与。
- **场景与人物**：社区活动报名；Emma、Lin和Mia。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Emma: The door is open. Come in.
  2. Lin: Thanks. Mia is outside.
  3. Emma: Mia, please go in with Lin.
  4. Mia: Okay. We are inside now.
  5. Emma: Get in the small car after registration.
  6. Lin: Where is the registration form?
  7. Emma: Fill in this form first.
  8. Lin: I fill it in at this desk.
  9. Mia: The group game starts soon. Join in.
  10. Lin: Great. We go in together and join in the activity.

#### Topic 06 · 外出与发现

- **教学说明**：学习用 `out` 表达出去、拿出、查明和外出用餐。
- **核心短语**：`go out, get out, take out, find out, eat out`
- **结构重点**：`take out` 可分；其他短语按完整单位使用。
- **完成标准**：能描述外出、离开空间、拿出物品和获得信息。
- **场景与人物**：Lin和Mia准备晚上外出。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Mia: The car is at the park. Let us get out.
  2. Lin: Okay. I get out carefully.
  3. Mia: Do you want to go out tonight?
  4. Lin: Yes. I do not want to stay home.
  5. Mia: Please take out the city guide.
  6. Lin: I take it out of my bag.
  7. Mia: We need to find out which restaurant is open.
  8. Lin: This guide has the information.
  9. Mia: Good. Let us eat out near the park.
  10. Lin: Great. We go out, find out the address, and eat out.

#### Topic 07 · 打开与继续

- **教学说明**：学习用 `on` 表达打开、穿上、登上交通工具、继续和短暂等待。
- **核心短语**：`turn on, put on, get on, carry on, hold on`
- **结构重点**：`turn on / put on` 可分；其余按完整短语使用。
- **完成标准**：能正确说出 `Turn it on. / Put it on. / Get on the bus.`。
- **场景与人物**：Lin和Mia准备乘车；Ben通过电话提醒。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Mia: The room is dark. Turn on the light.
  2. Lin: I turn it on.
  3. Mia: It is cold outside. Put on your coat.
  4. Lin: I put it on before we leave.
  5. Mia: The bus is here. Get on.
  6. Lin: Ben is on the phone.
  7. Ben: Hold on for a moment, Lin.
  8. Lin: Okay. I am on the bus.
  9. Ben: Please carry on with your trip.
  10. Lin: Thanks. We get on and carry on.

#### Topic 08 · 关闭与离开

- **教学说明**：学习用 `off` 表达关闭、脱下、下车、送达和出发。
- **核心短语**：`turn off, take off, get off, drop off, set off`
- **结构重点**：`turn off / take off / drop off` 可分；`get off / set off` 不拆开。
- **完成标准**：能在离开房间和交通场景中使用全部5个短语。
- **场景与人物**：Lin和Mia离开办公室去车站。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Mia: We leave now. Please turn off the computer.
  2. Lin: I turn it off.
  3. Mia: Take off your office badge.
  4. Lin: I take it off and put it away.
  5. Mia: We get off the bus at the station.
  6. Lin: Ben has a box for Emma.
  7. Mia: We drop it off at the front desk.
  8. Lin: What time do we set off?
  9. Mia: We set off at three.
  10. Lin: Everything is off. We are ready to set off.

### 🅲 返回、重新处理与物品操作

#### Topic 09 · 返回与归还

- **教学说明**：学习用 `back` 表达回来、回去、取回、带回和偿还。
- **核心短语**：`come back, go back, get back, bring back, pay back`
- **结构重点**：`bring back / pay back` 可分；前三个按完整移动短语使用。
- **完成标准**：能清楚表达人物和物品返回原处。
- **场景与人物**：Lin从社区中心回办公室归还物品。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Emma: Please come back before five.
  2. Lin: I come back after the activity.
  3. Emma: Then go back to the front desk.
  4. Lin: I get back to the office at four.
  5. Emma: Please bring back the blue folder.
  6. Lin: I bring it back with the box.
  7. Emma: Ben paid for your lunch.
  8. Lin: I need to pay him back.

#### Topic 10 · 拜访与重新处理

- **教学说明**：学习用 `over` 表达过来、让位、重新开始、仔细考虑和复习检查。
- **核心短语**：`come over, move over, start over, think over, go over`
- **结构重点**：五个短语在本话题均作为完整单位使用。
- **完成标准**：能邀请别人过来，并完成让位、重做和检查任务。
- **场景与人物**：Mia到Lin家一起检查活动计划。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Lin: Mia, please come over after lunch.
  2. Mia: Sure. I come over at two.
  3. Lin: This sofa is small. Please move over.
  4. Mia: No problem. There is space now.
  5. Lin: My first plan is not clear.
  6. Mia: Then start over with a clean page.
  7. Lin: I need to think over the new plan.
  8. Mia: We also need to go over the details.
  9. Lin: We start over and go over every part.
  10. Mia: Good. Think it over before the next meeting.

#### Topic 11 · 收纳与四处活动

- **教学说明**：学习用 `away / around` 表达收好、丢弃、离开和在周围查看或走动。
- **核心短语**：`put away, throw away, move away, look around, walk around`
- **结构重点**：`put away / throw away` 可分；其余按完整短语使用。
- **完成标准**：能整理物品并描述在一个地方四处活动。
- **场景与人物**：活动结束后，Lin和Mia整理场地。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Mia: Please put away the chairs.
  2. Lin: I put them away by the wall.
  3. Mia: Throw away these empty cups.
  4. Lin: I throw them away now.
  5. Mia: This table is in the way. Move away from it.
  6. Lin: Okay. The floor is clear.
  7. Mia: Look around for more paper.
  8. Lin: I see some near the door.
  9. Mia: Walk around the room one more time.
  10. Lin: I walk around, look around, and put everything away.

#### Topic 12 · 拿取与组合

- **教学说明**：学习拿起、放回、拆开、组合和交出物品。
- **核心短语**：`pick up, put back, take apart, put together, hand over`
- **结构重点**：5个短语都可带宾语；代词必须放在可分短语中间。
- **完成标准**：能正确说出 `Pick it up. / Put it back. / Hand it over.`。
- **场景与人物**：Ben和Lin整理一套展示设备。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Ben: Pick up the small box, please.
  2. Lin: I pick it up carefully.
  3. Ben: Take apart this simple stand.
  4. Lin: I take it apart on the table.
  5. Ben: Now put together the two main parts.
  6. Lin: I put them together.
  7. Ben: The cable belongs in this bag. Put it back.
  8. Lin: Everything is in the right place.
  9. Ben: Please hand over the final box to Emma.
  10. Lin: I hand it over after I put every part back.

### 🅳 信息、沟通与问题处理

#### Topic 13 · 查看与留意

- **教学说明**：学习查看、寻找、照看、浏览和提醒注意危险。
- **核心短语**：`look at, look for, look after, look through, watch out`
- **结构重点**：3个 `look + 介词` 短语不可拆开；`watch out` 可单独使用，也可接 `for + 人/物`。
- **完成标准**：能避免 `look it for / look it through` 等错误，并能用 `Watch out.` 及时提醒别人。
- **场景与人物**：Mia请Lin照看物品，并提醒他留意湿滑地面。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Mia: Look at this list, Lin.
  2. Lin: I look at every item.
  3. Mia: Please look for the blue folder.
  4. Lin: I look for it near the desk.
  5. Mia: I need to leave. Please look after my bag.
  6. Lin: No problem. I look after it.
  7. Mia: Look through the event guide before I leave.
  8. Lin: I look through it now.
  9. Mia: Watch out for the wet floor by the entrance.

## 附：小品词基础含义速查卡

| 小品词 | 基础感觉 | 本包示例 |
|:------|:---------|:---------|
| `up` | 向上、完成、改善 | `stand up, clean up, cheer up` |
| `down` | 向下、降低、停止 | `sit down, slow down, shut down` |
| `in` | 进入、加入 | `come in, fill in, join in` |
| `out` | 出去、显现、完全离开 | `go out, find out, take out` |
| `on` | 接触、开启、继续 | `put on, turn on, carry on` |
| `off` | 分离、关闭、离开 | `take off, turn off, get off` |
| `back` | 返回原处或原状态 | `come back, bring back, pay back` |
| `over` | 越过、重新、仔细检查 | `start over, think over, go over` |

## 附：宾语位置速查卡

### 1. 不带宾语

```text
Come in.
Sit down.
Please hurry up.
Ben shows up at six.
```

### 2. 可分短语

普通名词可以放在短语后面或中间：

```text
Turn on the light.
Turn the light on.

Pick up the box.
Pick the box up.
```

代词必须放在动词和小品词中间：

```text
Turn it on.       ✅
Turn on it.       ❌

Pick it up.       ✅
Pick up it.       ❌

Put them away.    ✅
Put away them.    ❌
```

### 3. 不可分短语

```text
Look for the key.
Look for it.

Look after Mia's bag.
Look after it.

Look through the guide.
Look through it.
```

不能写：

```text
Look it for.      ❌
Look it after.    ❌
Look it through.  ❌
```

## 输出训练流水线（Warmup Pipeline）

每个Topic的Warmup既覆盖5个核心短语，也必须让2个扩展短语完成实际输出。

| 阶段 | 训练目标 | 内容要求 |
|:----|:---------|:---------|
| **meaning_match** | 建立短语整体含义 | 核心5个 + 扩展2个 |
| **particle_choice** | 选择正确小品词 | 不用孤立规则替代短语记忆 |
| **chunk_shadowing** | 建立语音节奏 | 本Topic 10个核心句块 |
| **separability_check** | 判断可分、不可分或不带宾语 | 核心短语全部覆盖 |
| **noun_to_pronoun** | 普通名词改为 `it/him/her/them` | 所有可分和不可分短语 |
| **pattern_substitution** | 替换人物、宾语、地点和时间 | 本Topic 5个句型 |
| **extension_output** | 使用扩展短语完成构句 | 2个扩展短语各至少3题 |
| **dialogue_response** | 在真实对话中选择或说出短语 | 核心短语 + 已练扩展短语 |

### Warmup示例：Topic 07「打开与继续」

```json
{
  "core_phrases": ["turn on", "put on", "get on", "carry on", "hold on"],
  "extension_phrases": ["keep on", "move on"],
  "particle_choice": [
    "Turn ___ the light. -> on",
    "Put ___ your coat. -> on",
    "Get ___ the bus. -> on"
  ],
  "noun_to_pronoun": [
    "Turn on the light. -> Turn it on.",
    "Put on the coat. -> Put it on."
  ],
  "extension_output": [
    "Keep on with your work.",
    "The task is finished. Move on."
  ]
}
```

## 设计检查清单

```text
□ 全包正好16个Topic，每个Topic有独立教学说明和连续对话
□ 每个Topic固定5个新增核心短语和2个Warmup扩展短语
□ 同一个完整短语只在包④词表中登记一次
□ 前三包已学短语只进入复习池，不计入本包新增短语
□ 每个核心短语都有2个完整句块和1个句型
□ 每个核心短语至少在所属Topic对话中出现1次
□ 每个扩展短语至少进入3道Warmup输出题
□ 可分短语必须训练普通名词与代词位置
□ 不可分短语必须训练正确位置并配置错误辨析
□ 句型固定完整短语，不设计“___ + 小品词”的猜词框架
□ 每个短语只教一个最常用的基础含义
□ 不为增加数量加入低频、书面或复杂三词短语
```

### 常见设计错误

| 错误类型 | 问题 | 调整原则 |
|:---------|:-----|:---------|
| 把短语拆成两个词 | 用户认识单词却不会整体使用 | 将 `pick up` 作为一个词条 |
| 只有中文释义 | 用户不知道真实句子怎么说 | 每个核心短语配置2个完整句块 |
| 代词位置错误 | 出现 `pick up it` | 可分短语强制训练 `pick it up` |
| 把不可分短语拆开 | 出现 `look it for` | 训练 `look for it` |
| 一个短语塞多个含义 | L1学习负担过大 | 本包只保留一个最高频含义 |
| 对话机械罗列 | 短语出现但交流不自然 | 使用固定人物和连续任务串联 |

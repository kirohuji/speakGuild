# Course ③ · 海外独立生活（L3）

> 把基础旅游英语升级为能够独立办理长期生活事务。

## 学习包概览

| 维度 | 设计 |
|:-----|:-----|
| CEFR | B1 |
| 前置要求 | Course ①—② |
| 核心目标 | 说明需求、询问信息、比较选择、确认条件并保留记录 |
| 结课任务 | 独立处理一项包含咨询、选择、办理和跟进的生活事务 |

## 场景与 Topics

| 场景 | Topics | 交际成果 |
|:-----|:-------|:---------|
| 租房入住 | 01 说明住房需求；02 看房提问；03 确认合同条件；04 报修沟通 | 完成入住和基础维护 |
| 医疗健康 | 05 预约；06 描述症状经过；07 听懂建议；08 药物与复诊 | 完成非急诊就医 |
| 银行账单 | 09 开户与验证；10 解释交易；11 询问费用；12 处理账单差异 | 管理日常财务事务 |
| 交通出行 | 13 规划路线；14 票务规则；15 延误改签；16 租车与保险 | 应对计划变化 |
| 政务社区 | 17 填表和材料；18 电话咨询；19 预约办理；20 社区资源 | 获取公共服务 |

## 逐 Topic 完整教学设计

> 下列内容是生成 CSV、Warmup 与 Ink 的权威 Topic 契约。Vocabulary 只登记词项；完整表达进入 Chunk；带槽位的生成结构进入 Pattern。每个 Topic 均复用：Foundation 2、7、9、10 的需求、礼貌请求、比较和间接问句。

### Topic 01 · 说明住房需求

- **教学说明**：在“finding an affordable apartment”情境中学习说明住房需求，重点不是复述范句，而是推动交流得到明确结果。
- **核心词（Vocabulary）**：`appointment`, `document`, `requirement`, `fee`。
- **扩展词（Extension）**：`eligibility`, `availability`。
- **核心句块（Chunks）**：`I'm looking for something within my budget.`；`I'd like to clarify one condition.`；`So, just to confirm, the fee includes everything.`；`I'm calling to follow up on my request.`。
- **句型（Patterns）**：`I'm looking for something that ___.`；`I'd like to clarify ___.`。
- **完成标准**：能在陌生变体中完成 12—16 轮互动，主动使用至少 3 个核心句块、1 个主句型，并完成追问、澄清或确认。
- **口语输出**：围绕“finding an affordable apartment”完成 2—5 分钟未准备任务；不得照读对话。
- **微写作**：写 120—250 词，把同一能力迁移为咨询、确认或跟进邮件。
- **反馈与重做**：第一次输出后按任务完成、连贯、词汇、语法及发音/拼写反馈；不看完整范文完成第二次重说和重写，7 天后换情境复测。
- **跨包复习**：Foundation 2、7、9、10 的需求、礼貌请求、比较和间接问句；本 Topic 新表达须在本包后续至少两个 Topic 再次主动调用。
- **具体对话**：
  1. Staff: How can I help you with finding an affordable apartment?
  2. Lin: I'm looking for something within my budget.
  3. Staff: Certainly. What information do you need first?
  4. Lin: Could you tell me what documents are required?
  5. Staff: I can explain that. Is there a particular condition you want to check?
  6. Lin: I'd like to clarify one condition.
  7. Staff: The usual process takes three working days.
  8. Lin: What happens if the appointment is delayed?
  9. Staff: We will contact you if anything changes.
  10. Lin: So, just to confirm, the fee includes everything.
  11. Staff: Is there anything else I can confirm?
  12. Lin: Could you confirm the next step in writing?

- **跨包复习**：Foundation 2、7、9、10 的需求、礼貌请求、比较和间接问句；本 Topic 新表达须在本包后续至少两个 Topic 再次主动调用。
- **具体对话**：
  1. Staff: How can I help you with finding a community service?
  2. Lin: I'm looking for something within my budget.
  3. Staff: Certainly. What information do you need first?
  4. Lin: Could you tell me what documents are required?
  5. Staff: I can explain that. Is there a particular condition you want to check?
  6. Lin: I'd like to clarify one condition.
  7. Staff: The usual process takes three working days.
  8. Lin: What happens if the appointment is delayed?
  9. Staff: We will contact you if anything changes.
  10. Lin: So, just to confirm, the fee includes everything.
  11. Staff: Is there anything else I can confirm?
  12. Lin: Could you confirm the next step in writing?

## 核心语言系统

- 间接问句：`Could you tell me whether...?`
- 条件确认：`What happens if...?`
- 需求限定：`I'm looking for something that...`
- 复述确认：`So, just to confirm...`
- 跟进：`I'm calling to follow up on...`

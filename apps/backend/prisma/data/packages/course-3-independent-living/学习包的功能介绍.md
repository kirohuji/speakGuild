### 本包新语法

> Foundation 已封版。以下中级语法通过本包的交际场景首次系统引入。

| 语法点 | 本包中的场景应用 | 核心句式 |
|:-------|:----------------|:---------|
| **使役结构 have / get sth done** | 描述"让别人做/安排做"的生活事务 | `I **had my phone repaired**.` / `I need to **get my hair cut**.` |
| **被动语态入门**（一般现在/过去） | 报修、收件、填表中被动无处不在 | `The package **was delivered** this morning.` / `The form **must be filled out**.` |

> 以上语法在本包的 Chunks 和 Patterns 中体现，并在后续 Course 4—10 中持续复用。

### Topic 01 · 说明住房需求

- **教学说明**：在"finding an affordable apartment"情境中学习说明住房需求。本Topic聚焦：Clearly stating requirements and constraints。
- **核心词（Vocabulary）**：`appointment`,`document`,`requirement`,`fee`。
- **扩展词（Extension）**：`eligibility`,`availability`。
- **核心句块（Chunks）**：`I am looking for a one-bedroom apartment within walking distance of the station.`；`My budget is fairly tight, so I need something under eight hundred a month.`；`Do you have any units available that match those requirements?`；`I would also need the place to be furnished, if possible.`。
- **句型（Patterns）**：`I am looking for ___.`；`My budget is ___, so I need ___.`。
- **完成标准**：能在陌生变体中完成 12—16 轮互动，使用至少 3 个核心句块和 1 个主句型，并形成结论、决定或下一步。
- **口语输出**：围绕"finding an affordable apartment"完成 2—5 分钟未准备任务；不得照读对话。
- **微写作**：写 120—250 词，内容必须重新组织，不得抄写口语稿。

- **具体对话**：
  1. Staff: Good morning. How can I help you with your apartment search?
  2. Lin: I am looking for a one-bedroom apartment within walking distance of the station. My budget is tight, so I need something under eight hundred a month.
  3. Staff: I have a few options. Any other requirements?
  4. Lin: I would also need the place to be furnished, if possible. And I prefer a quiet building.
  5. Staff: That narrows it down. Let me show you two units that match.
  6. Lin: Do you have any units available right now? I am hoping to move in by the end of the month.

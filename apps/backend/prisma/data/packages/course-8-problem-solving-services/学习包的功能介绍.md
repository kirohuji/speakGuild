# Course ⑧ · 问题解决与服务沟通（L4）

> 从简单报错或投诉，升级为清楚描述复杂问题、说明影响、评估方案并持续跟进。

## 学习包概览

| 维度 | 设计 |
|:-----|:-----|
| CEFR | B1+ |
| 前置要求 | Course ①—⑦ |
| 核心目标 | 在出现责任不清或多次变化时，仍能冷静推动问题解决 |
| 结课任务 | 完成一次“首次报告—方案讨论—升级处理—结果确认”的服务任务 |
### 本包新语法

> Foundation 已封版。以下中级语法通过本包的交际场景首次系统引入。

| 语法点 | 本包中的场景应用 | 核心句式 |
|:-------|:----------------|:---------|
| **被动语态综合运用** | 投诉、保修、服务跟进中精确描述状态 | `It **wasn't fixed** properly.` / `My refund **hasn't been processed** yet.` |
| **量词系统深入** | 描述问题的严重程度、资源、数量和频率 | `There's **too much** noise.` / `We have **very little** time.` / `**A few** items are missing.` |

> 以上语法在本包的 Chunks 和 Patterns 中体现，并在后续 Course 9—10 中持续复用。
## 场景与 Topics

| 场景 | Topics | 交际成果 |
|:-----|:-------|:---------|
| 描述问题 | 01 当前现象；02 发生经过；03 已尝试措施；04 具体影响 | 建立完整问题记录 |
| 明确责任 | 05 确认政策；06 询问负责方；07 区分事实与假设；08 提供证据 | 避免无效争论 |
| 讨论方案 | 09 请求解决；10 比较补救方案；11 说明可接受范围；12 确认时间 | 选择可行补救 |
| 升级处理 | 13 礼貌坚持；14 请求转交；15 复述历史；16 回应拒绝 | 在僵局中推进 |
| 跟进闭环 | 17 查询进度；18 处理新变化；19 确认问题解决；20 提供反馈 | 完成服务闭环 |

## 逐 Topic 完整教学设计

> 下列内容是生成 CSV、Warmup 与 Ink 的权威 Topic 契约。Vocabulary、Chunk、Pattern 分开登记；每个 Topic 均复用：Course 3、6、7 的服务事务、证据核实和方案协商。

### Topic 01 · 当前现象

- **教学说明**：在“an internet service failure”情境中学习当前现象，必须处理信息缺口、限制或追问。
- **核心词（Vocabulary）**：`issue`, `fault`, `damage`, `impact`。
- **扩展词（Extension）**：`compensation`, `supervisor`。
- **核心句块（Chunks）**：`The issue started when the service stopped unexpectedly.`；`This has resulted in extra cost and delay.`；`What I'd like you to do is arrange a replacement.`；`Could you confirm what will happen next?`。
- **句型（Patterns）**：`The issue started when ___.`；`This has resulted in ___.`。
- **完成标准**：能在陌生变体中完成 12—16 轮互动，使用至少 3 个核心句块和 1 个主句型，并形成结论、决定或下一步。
- **口语输出**：围绕“an internet service failure”完成 3—5 分钟未准备任务，并回答至少 2 个追问。
- **微写作**：写 180—250 词问题记录、投诉或跟进邮件；内容必须重新组织，不得抄写口语稿。
- **反馈与重做**：按任务完成、结构、词汇、语法及发音/拼写反馈；完成第二次重说和重写，7 天后使用新材料或新限制复测。
- **跨包复习**：Course 3、6、7 的服务事务、证据核实和方案协商；本 Topic 新表达须在本包后续至少两个 Topic 再次主动调用。
- **具体对话**：
  1. Agent: Please describe the problem with an internet service failure.
  2. Lin: The issue started when the service stopped unexpectedly.
  3. Agent: Have you contacted us before?
  4. Lin: Since then, I have contacted support twice.
  5. Agent: What impact has this had?
  6. Lin: This has resulted in extra cost and delay.
  7. Agent: Do you have supporting records?
  8. Lin: I can provide the receipt and photographs.
  9. Agent: What solution would be acceptable?
  10. Lin: What I'd like you to do is arrange a replacement.
  11. Agent: I will record that and arrange the next step.
  12. Lin: Could you confirm what will happen next?

  1. Agent: Please describe the problem with final feedback on the service.
  2. Lin: The issue started when the service stopped unexpectedly.
  3. Agent: Have you contacted us before?
  4. Lin: Since then, I have contacted support twice.
  5. Agent: What impact has this had?
  6. Lin: This has resulted in extra cost and delay.
  7. Agent: Do you have supporting records?
  8. Lin: I can provide the receipt and photographs.
  9. Agent: What solution would be acceptable?
  10. Lin: What I'd like you to do is arrange a replacement.
  11. Agent: I will record that and arrange the next step.
  12. Lin: Could you confirm what will happen next?

## 核心语言系统

- 问题链：`The issue started when...` / `Since then...`
- 影响：`This has resulted in...`
- 请求：`What I'd like you to do is...`
- 坚持：`I understand the policy; however...`
- 跟进：`Could you confirm what will happen next?`

### Topic 01 · 分析听众

- **教学说明**：在"a presentation for new students"情境中学习分析听众。本Topic聚焦：Understanding who you are speaking to。
- **核心词（Vocabulary）**：`audience`，`students`，`jargon`，`basics`。

- **扩展词（Extension）**：`topic`，`avoid`；补充词：`needs`，`year`。

- **核心句块（Chunks）**：`Before I prepare anything, I need to understand who will be in the audience.`；`These are first-year students with no background in the topic.`；`I should avoid jargon and start with the basics.`；`The presentation needs to answer their main question: why does this matter to me?`。
- **句型（Patterns）**：`Before I prepare, I need to understand ___.`；`The presentation needs to answer: ___.`。
- **完成标准**：能在陌生变体中完成 12—16 轮互动，使用至少 3 个核心句块和 1 个主句型，并形成结论、决定或下一步。
- **口语输出**：围绕"a presentation for new students"完成 2—5 分钟未准备任务；不得照读对话。
- **微写作**：写 120—250 词，内容必须重新组织，不得抄写口语稿。
- **反馈与重做**：按任务完成、结构、词汇、语法及发音/拼写反馈；完成第二次重说和重写，7 天后换情境复测。
- **跨包复习**：Course 1、2、6、7 的论点、叙事、信息综合和决策；本 Topic 新表达须在本包后续至少两个 Topic 再次主动调用。



- **词汇扩展（Vocabulary Expansion）**：
  - `captivating` (adj) — A truly captivating opening is absolutely essential for a memorable and effective presentation.
  - `polished` (adj) — The entire presentation was impressively polished and thoroughly professional in every respect.
  - `cumbersome` (adj) — Avoid using cumbersome slides that are overloaded with far too much dense text.
  - `impactful` (adj) — An impactful and memorable closing statement leaves a genuinely lasting impression on the audience.
  - `memorable` (adj) — The most memorable presentations tell a compelling story, not merely present dry data.
  - `seamless` (adj) — The transition between the main sections should feel completely seamless and natural.
  - `deck` (n) — I will circulate the complete presentation deck to everyone after the meeting concludes.
  - `demo` (n) — A compelling live demo of the prototype generated by far the most audience engagement.
  - `flow` (n) — The entire presentation had a wonderfully natural flow from one section to the next.
  - `recap` (n) — Here is a quick recap of the three main recommendations we have discussed today.
  - `walkthrough` (n) — Let me give you a rapid walkthrough of the key new dashboard features.

- **具体对话**：
  1. Emma: Lin, you are giving a presentation to new students. Who is your audience?
  2. Lin: First-year students with no background in the topic. I should avoid jargon and start with the basics.
  3. Emma: What is their main concern?
  4. Lin: They want to know why this matters to them. The presentation needs to answer that question in the first two minutes.

  5. Emma: That was handout. Your use of coherent made it memorable.

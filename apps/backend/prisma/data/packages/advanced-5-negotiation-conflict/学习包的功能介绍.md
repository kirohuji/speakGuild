# Advanced ⑤ · 高分歧协商与冲突修复（L4）

> 从一般协商升级为在利益冲突、情绪压力和信任受损时仍保持有效沟通。

## 学习包概览

| 维度 | 设计 |
|:-----|:-----|
| CEFR | B2 |
| 前置要求 | Advanced ①—④ |
| 核心目标 | 区分立场与真实需求，降低对抗并形成可持续协议 |
| 结课任务 | 处理一次多方冲突，形成协议并修复后续合作关系 |

## 场景与 Topics

| 场景 | Topics | 交际成果 |
|:-----|:-------|:---------|
| 诊断冲突 | 01 区分事实与解释；02 识别利益；03 识别情绪；04 确认共同目标 | 找到真实问题 |
| 降低对抗 | 05 承认影响；06 重构指责；07 设置对话规则；08 暂停升级 | 恢复可谈状态 |
| 探索方案 | 09 提问真实需求；10 创造多个选项；11 测试交换条件；12 评估公平性 | 扩大解决空间 |
| 处理僵局 | 13 说明不可接受点；14 引入客观标准；15 寻求第三方；16 暂定安排 | 避免谈判破裂 |
| 修复信任 | 17 真诚道歉；18 说明补救；19 建立验证点；20 回顾协议 | 维持长期关系 |

## 逐 Topic 完整教学设计

> 下列契约用于生成 Advanced CSV、Warmup 与 Ink。每个 Topic 都要求精确语域、异议处理、反馈后修订，并复用：Course 4、7、8 与 Advanced 1、4 的关系、协商、问题处理和领导沟通。

### Topic 01 · 区分事实与解释

- **教学说明**：在“separating observed events from interpretations”中学习区分事实与解释；必须处理限定、异议、隐含假设或关系风险。
- **核心词（Vocabulary）**：`conflict`, `interpretation`, `interest`, `emotion`。
- **扩展词（Extension）**：`impasse`, `goodwill`。
- **核心句块（Chunks）**：`The way I understood the situation was different from your account.`；`I can acknowledge the impact without agreeing with that interpretation.`；`What would a fair outcome look like from your perspective?`；`I should have communicated the risk much earlier.`。
- **句型（Patterns）**：`The way I understood ___ was ___.`；`Can we separate ___ from ___?`。
- **完成标准**：完成 16—24 轮复杂互动，灵活改写至少一次，回应一个有根据的异议，并形成精确结论或修订方案。
- **口语输出**：围绕“separating observed events from interpretations”完成 4—6 分钟未准备表达，并接受至少 3 个追问。
- **微写作**：写 250—350 词多段文本，控制语域、限定主张并回应一个反例；不得照抄口语稿。
- **反馈与重做**：依据任务、结构、精确度、语域、语法、发音/拼写反馈；完成第二版重说和重写，7—14 天后换立场或受众复测。
- **跨包复习**：Course 4、7、8 与 Advanced 1、4 的关系、协商、问题处理和领导沟通；本 Topic 新表达须在本包后续至少两个 Topic 再次调用。
- **具体对话**：
  1. Mediator: How do you understand the conflict around separating observed events from interpretations?
  2. Lin: The way I understood the situation was different from your account.
  3. Mediator: What need lies behind your position?
  4. Lin: What matters most to us is having a predictable process.
  5. Mediator: The other side says your team ignored the impact.
  6. Lin: I can acknowledge the impact without agreeing with that interpretation.
  7. Mediator: How can we reduce the scope of the disagreement?
  8. Lin: Can we separate the immediate issue from the wider relationship?
  9. Mediator: What would fairness mean here?
  10. Lin: What would a fair outcome look like from your perspective?
  11. Mediator: Is any part of your position non-negotiable?
  12. Lin: This requirement is essential, but the timing is negotiable.
  13. Mediator: What would you change after this discussion?
  14. Lin: This requirement is essential, but the timing is negotiable.
  15. Mediator: Please state the revised position clearly.
  16. Lin: Let's review the agreement after two weeks and adjust if necessary.

## 核心语言系统

`The way I understood the situation was...` / `What matters most to us is...` / `Can we separate the immediate issue from...?` / `What would a fair outcome look like?`

## 章节与剧集

从双人分歧过渡到多方冲突；每章包含情绪变化，但角色不能被写成简单的“坏人”。

## 数据目标

5 Scenes / 20 Topics / 约 90 Chunks / 约 45 Patterns / 150—180 Vocabulary / 10 Episodes / 20 Ink scripts。

## 设计边界

- 不模拟暴力、威胁或需要专业危机干预的场景。
- 不把压制对方视为成功。
- 通过标准包括任务协议和关系修复两个维度。

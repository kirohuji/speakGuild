> 从理解报道内容，升级为识别框架、比较叙述方式，并有分寸地讨论公共议题。

## 学习包概览

| 维度 | 设计 |
|:-----|:-----|

## 逐 Topic 完整教学设计

> 下列契约用于生成 Advanced CSV、Warmup 与 Ink。每个 Topic 都要求多来源或多立场处理、精确语域、反馈后修订，并复用：Course 6 与 Advanced 1、3、6 的信息、限定、论证和证据综合。

### Topic 01 · 标题选择

- **教学说明**：在“two headlines about the same housing report”中学习标题选择；需要综合多个来源或立场，并显式说明限制。
- **核心词（Vocabulary）**：`headline`, `frame`, `emphasis`, `source`。
- **扩展词（Extension）**：`framing`, `sensational`。
- **核心句块（Chunks）**：`The headline frames the issue primarily as a question of cost.`；`Both reports agree on the figures but emphasise different causes.`；`Reasonable people may disagree about the balance of fairness and efficiency.`；`The most reliable facts are those confirmed by both accounts.`。
- **句型（Patterns）**：`The report frames ___ as ___.`；`Both accounts agree on ___ but differ on ___.`。
- **完成标准**：完成 16—24 轮复杂互动，综合至少两项信息，回应一个实质异议，主动改写并形成有限定的结论。
- **口语输出**：围绕“two headlines about the same housing report”完成 6—10 分钟表达或答辩，处理至少 3 个不可预先知道的追问。
- **微写作**：写 300—500 词报道比较与立场分析，控制结构、语域、证据归属和结论强度；不得照抄口语稿。
- **反馈与重做**：依据任务、结构、证据、精确度、语域、语法、发音/拼写反馈；完成第二版，7—14 天后更换受众、材料或立场复测。
- **跨包复习**：Course 6 与 Advanced 1、3、6 的信息、限定、论证和证据综合；本 Topic 的新表达须在本包后续至少两个 Topic 再次主动调用。
- **具体对话**：
  1. Editor: How is two headlines about the same housing report being framed?
  2. Lin: The headline frames the issue primarily as a question of cost.
  3. Editor: Which perspective is receiving less attention?
  4. Lin: What receives less attention is the effect on part-time workers.
  5. Editor: What do the reports agree on?
  6. Lin: Both reports agree on the figures but emphasise different causes.
  7. Editor: How does source selection affect interpretation?
  8. Lin: The choice of sources shapes how responsibility is understood.
  9. Editor: Why might informed people still disagree?
  10. Lin: Reasonable people may disagree about the balance of fairness and efficiency.
  11. Editor: Which facts are most reliable?
  12. Lin: The most reliable facts are those confirmed by both accounts.
  13. Editor: What uncertainty still needs to be communicated?
  14. Lin: The most reliable facts are those confirmed by both accounts.
  15. Editor: Give us your final, carefully limited conclusion.
  16. Lin: My position is provisional because one key perspective is still missing.

## 核心语言系统

`The report frames the issue as...` / `What receives less attention is...` / `Both accounts agree on..., but differ in...` / `Reasonable people may disagree about...`

## 章节与剧集

材料全部采用原创或模拟文本；议题以城市生活、教育、工作变化、科技和环境等通用主题为主。

## 数据目标

5 Scenes / 20 Topics / 约 90 Chunks / 约 50 Patterns / 160—180 Vocabulary / 10 Episodes / 20 Ink scripts。

## 设计边界

- 不灌输特定政治立场。
- 不复制真实新闻全文。
- 学习者可以保留判断，但必须说明已知、未知和依据。

# 基础② · 日常生活与一般动词（L1）

## 学习包概览

本学习包是 **基础系列（Foundation）· 第 2 包**。学习者在包①已经能用 be 动词完成寒暄、介绍和基础判断，本包开始进入日常交流真正的“动作层”——说自己喜欢什么、想要什么、需要什么、每天做什么，并围绕饮食、日常用品、购物和联系约见完成常见生活对话。

本包以 `like / want / need / would like` 为四条主线，同时建立第一、二人称一般现在时的语感。不同场景不是各教一套互不相关的内容，而是让目标动词在生活中反复出现、逐步变得自然。

> 🎯 **核心目标**：学完本包，学习者能使用一般动词描述自己的生活，询问对方的喜好和日常，准确表达“喜欢、想要、需要”，并能在咖啡店、商店和约见等常见场景中使用 `would like` 作出礼貌选择。

> ✅ **结课能力**：独立介绍自己的日常和喜好，完成一次简短点单或购物对话，并通过电话或消息与朋友确认一次见面安排。

### 为什么一般动词如此重要？

be 动词帮助学习者说“我是谁、怎么样、在哪里”，一般动词则让学习者说出生活中实际发生的动作：

- `I like tea.`
- `I need some water.`
- `I want to go home.`
- `I work during the day.`
- `Do you have a charger?`

中文中的否定和提问不需要改变句子结构，而英语一般动词需要借助 `do / don't`：

> ❌ 常见错误：*I not like coffee. / *You need help? / *What you do at night?
> ✅ 正确表达：**I don't like coffee. / Do you need help? / What do you do at night?**

本包通过大量真实问答和人称转换，让学习者建立以下语感：

```text
I like...        → I don't like...        → Do you like...?
I need...        → I don't need...        → Do you need...?
I work...        → I don't work...        → Do you work...?
```

### 和第一包的关系

| 第一包：状态与判断 | 第二包：动作与需求 |
|:------------------|:------------------|
| `I'm tired.` | `I need a break.` |
| `It's my phone.` | `I need my phone.` |
| `Are you ready?` | `Do you want to go?` |
| `It's a coffee.` | `I'd like a coffee.` |
| 说“是什么、怎么样” | 说“喜欢、想要、需要、做什么” |

第一包的高频物品、状态和礼貌词会自然复用，但本包的学习重点是用一般动词推动交流。

### 覆盖核心语法

| 语法点 | 训练重点 | 高频表达示例 |
|:------|:---------|:-------------|
| 一般动词肯定句 | `I/You/We + 动词原形` | `I work during the day.` |
| 一般动词否定句 | `don't + 动词原形` | `I don't drink coffee.` |
| 一般疑问句 | `Do you + 动词原形...?` | `Do you like music?` |
| 简短回答 | `Yes, I do. / No, I don't.` | 喜好、需求与日常问答 |
| 特殊疑问句 | `What/Where/When do you...?` | `What do you need?` |
| 喜好表达 | `like + 名词 / like to + 动词` | `I like to read.` |
| 意愿表达 | `want + 名词 / want to + 动词` | `I want a new laptop.` |
| 需求表达 | `need + 名词 / need to + 动词` | `I need to leave.` |
| 礼貌选择 | `would like + 名词 / to + 动词` | `I'd like some water.` |
| 拥有与询问 | `have / Do you have...?` | `Do you have a charger?` |
| 时间搭配 | `in / at / on / after / every` | `I read at night.` |
| 可数与不可数基础 | `a/an / some / any` | `a ticket / some water` |
| 常用缩写 | `don't / I'd / you're` | 口语识别与自然输出 |

> 本包的输出始终以 `I / you / we` 为主。第三人称一般动词变化会在基础③集中训练。

## 设计哲学

### 🗣️ 高频生活交流优先

本包不是把“一般现在时”拆成孤立规则，而是从日常生活中真正高频的表达出发：

- `I like this one.`
- `I don't need it.`
- `Do you want some coffee?`
- `I'd like a small one, please.`
- `I get up at seven.`
- `Do you have a minute?`
- `Let's get together at six.`
- `I'm on my way.`

语法服务于生活表达，场景服务于核心能力，不为了场景数量塞入低频词汇。

### 🧩 两层词汇分配策略

| 层级 | 定义 | 使用位置 |
|:----|:-----|:---------|
| **核心词（Core）** | 本话题中必须主动掌握的高频词 | 进入核心句块与句型示例 |
| **扩展词（Extension）** | 能自然替换进同一句型、扩大表达范围的词 | 进入 `warmup_pipeline` |

例如在“我需要什么”中，`need, help, time, break` 是核心词；`charger, umbrella, ticket` 可以作为扩展词进入 `I need ___` 的输出训练。

### 🔗 累加式设计（Cumulative Design）

```text
表达喜好
→ 表达想要和需要
→ 在饮食场景中礼貌选择
→ 描述自己一天的动作
→ 在日常购物中询问和决定
→ 联系朋友并完成约见
```

四条核心表达会跨场景自然复现：

| 核心表达 | 前段建立 | 后段应用 |
|:---------|:---------|:---------|
| `like` | 食物与活动喜好 | 选择商品、选择见面活动 |
| `want` | 想要物品与行动 | 购物决定、约见意愿 |
| `need` | 实际需求 | 日常用品、时间和帮助 |
| `would like` | 礼貌选择 | 点单、购物和邀请回应 |

### 📐 句块、句型与场景协同

| 高频句块 | 可迁移句型 | 真实应用 |
|:---------|:-----------|:---------|
| `I don't drink coffee.` | `I don't ___.` | 说习惯或拒绝 |
| `I need a little more time.` | `I need ___.` | 表达实际需求 |
| `I'd like a table for two.` | `I'd like ___.` | 点单和选择 |
| `Do you have this in blue?` | `Do you have ___?` | 询问商品或物品 |
| `Let's get together at six.` | `Let's get together at ___.` | 联系约见 |

## 逐话题教学说明与对话 List

本节对应 `training_topics.csv` 的教学说明，并为剧集、Warmup 和角色扮演提供最小对话原型。每个 Topic 的对话都必须标明场景和人物。

### 🅰️ 喜好与选择

#### Topic 01 · 喜欢的吃喝

- **教学说明**：学习用 `I like...` 表达饮食喜好，并用常见食物和饮品完成替换。
- **学习重点**：`like + 名词`；不可数饮品前通常不直接加 `a`。
- **完成标准**：能说出三种喜欢的吃喝，并听懂别人的选择。
- **对话场景**：课间，两位学员聊早餐饮品。
- **人物**：Lin；同学 Mia。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Mia: I like coffee. How about you?
  2. Lin: I like tea.
  3. Mia: Do you like milk?
  4. Lin: Yes, I do.
  5. Mia: Do you drink milk every morning?
  6. Lin: No, I don't. I usually drink tea.
  7. Mia: What food do you like?
  8. Lin: I prefer noodles.
  9. Mia: I like noodles too.
  10. Lin: Great. We like the same food.

#### Topic 02 · 喜欢的活动

- **教学说明**：学习用 `like to + 动词` 和一般动词表达阅读、音乐、电影与运动喜好。
- **学习重点**：区分 `I like music` 和 `I like to read`。
- **完成标准**：能介绍两项爱好，并询问对方喜欢做什么。
- **对话场景**：语言交换活动的兴趣配对。
- **人物**：Lin；搭档 Alex。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Alex: What do you like to do?
  2. Lin: I like to read.
  3. Alex: I like to watch movies.
  4. Lin: Great. Me too.
  5. Alex: Do you listen to music?
  6. Lin: Yes, I do. I listen to music every day.
  7. Alex: Do you play sports?
  8. Lin: No, I don't.
  9. Lin: What do you do on weekends?
  10. Alex: I watch movies and play games.

#### Topic 03 · 喜欢还是不喜欢

- **教学说明**：学习询问喜好、作简短回答并表达不喜欢。
- **学习重点**：`Do you like...? / Yes, I do / No, I don't / I don't like...`。
- **完成标准**：能完成至少四轮肯定与否定喜好问答。
- **对话场景**：朋友一起选择午餐。
- **人物**：Lin；朋友 Sofia。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Sofia: Do you like spicy food?
  2. Lin: No, I don't.
  3. Lin: Do you like noodles?
  4. Sofia: Yes, I do. Noodles are my favorite.
  5. Lin: Do you like rice too?
  6. Sofia: Yes, but I prefer noodles.
  7. Sofia: What's your favorite food?
  8. Lin: My favorite food is soup.
  9. Sofia: Do you like hot soup?
  10. Lin: Yes, I do, but I don't like spicy soup.

### 🅱️ 想要与需要

#### Topic 04 · 我想要什么

- **教学说明**：学习用 `want + 名词` 直接表达想要的食物、饮品、物品或票。
- **学习重点**：可数名词前的 `a/one` 和不可数名词前的 `some`。
- **完成标准**：能根据场景说出自己想要的具体事物。
- **对话场景**：车站售票窗口。
- **人物**：乘客 Lin；售票员 Emma。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Emma: Hello. What do you want?
  2. Lin: I want one ticket, please.
  3. Emma: One ticket to Central Station?
  4. Lin: Yes, please.
  5. Emma: Do you want a morning ticket?
  6. Lin: No, I want an afternoon ticket.
  7. Emma: The two o'clock ticket?
  8. Lin: Yes, that's right.
  9. Emma: Here is your ticket.
  10. Lin: Thank you.

#### Topic 05 · 我想做什么

- **教学说明**：学习用 `want to + 动词` 表达想做、想去或想参加的事情。
- **学习重点**：`want to` 后使用动词原形。
- **完成标准**：能表达三个行动意愿，并邀请对方一起行动。
- **对话场景**：下课后讨论接下来的安排。
- **人物**：Lin；同学 Ben。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Lin: I want to go home.
  2. Ben: Do you want to eat first?
  3. Lin: Yes. Do you want to come with me?
  4. Ben: Sure.
  5. Ben: Where do you want to eat?
  6. Lin: I want to try the new noodle shop.
  7. Ben: Great. I want to try it too.
  8. Lin: Do you want to walk there?
  9. Ben: Yes. It's near the station.
  10. Lin: Okay. Let's go together.

#### Topic 06 · 我真正需要什么

- **教学说明**：学习用 `need + 名词` 和 `need to + 动词` 表达实际需要，并与 `want` 区分。
- **学习重点**：`need help/time` 与 `need to leave/sleep`。
- **完成标准**：能根据真实情况表达一项需求和一项必要行动。
- **对话场景**：学习活动进行到一半，Lin 需要帮助和更多时间。
- **人物**：学员 Lin；助教 Mia。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Mia: Do you need anything?
  2. Lin: Yes, I need some help.
  3. Lin: I also need a little more time.
  4. Mia: No problem.
  5. Mia: Do you need help with this question?
  6. Lin: Yes, I don't understand this word.
  7. Mia: Okay. I can explain it.
  8. Lin: Thank you. I need to finish this today.
  9. Mia: Take your time.
  10. Lin: Thanks. I think I can finish now.

### 🅲 饮食与点单

#### Topic 07 · 礼貌点单

- **教学说明**：学习在咖啡店或简餐店使用 `I'd like...` 和 `Can I have...?` 礼貌点单。
- **学习重点**：`would like` 比 `want` 更适合直接对服务人员表达选择。
- **完成标准**：能点一杯饮品和一份食物，并正确使用 `please`。
- **对话场景**：社区咖啡店柜台。
- **人物**：顾客 Lin；店员 Emma。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Emma: Hi. What would you like?
  2. Lin: I'd like a table for two and some tea, please.
  3. Lin: I'd also like to order the chicken salad.
  4. Emma: Sure.
  5. Emma: Would you like the tea hot or cold?
  6. Lin: Hot, please.
  7. Emma: Would you like anything else?
  8. Lin: Yes. Can I have some water?
  9. Emma: Of course. That's one tea, one salad and some water.
  10. Lin: That's right. Thank you.

#### Topic 08 · 接受、拒绝与二选一

- **教学说明**：学习回应别人的食物邀请，在两项选择之间作决定，并礼貌表示“够了”。
- **学习重点**：`Would you like...? / Yes, please / No, thank you / That's enough`。
- **完成标准**：能自然接受一次、拒绝一次并完成一次二选一。
- **对话场景**：朋友在家招待 Lin 喝下午茶。
- **人物**：主人 Sofia；客人 Lin。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Sofia: Would you like some coffee?
  2. Lin: No, thank you.
  3. Sofia: Tea or juice?
  4. Lin: Tea, please.
  5. Sofia: Would you like any more cake?
  6. Lin: No, that's enough. Thank you.
  7. Sofia: How about some fruit?
  8. Lin: Yes, please. I'd like an apple.
  9. Sofia: Here you are.
  10. Lin: Thanks. The tea tastes great.

#### Topic 09 · 追加与结账

- **教学说明**：学习在用餐过程中追加水或餐具，并在结束时索要账单。
- **学习重点**：`Can I have some more...?` 和 `Can I have the bill, please?`。
- **完成标准**：能提出两项追加请求并完成结账。
- **对话场景**：简餐店餐桌旁。
- **人物**：顾客 Lin；服务员 Ben。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Lin: Excuse me. Can I have some more water?
  2. Ben: Of course.
  3. Lin: And can I have a fork, please?
  4. Ben: Sure.
  5. Lin: Can I have the bill, please?
  6. Ben: Yes. Would you like anything else first?
  7. Lin: No, thank you. That's enough.
  8. Ben: Okay. The bill is eighty yuan.
  9. Lin: Can I pay in cash?
  10. Ben: Yes, you can.

### 🅳 我的一天

#### Topic 10 · 早晨

- **教学说明**：学习按时间顺序描述起床、早餐和出门三个高频早晨动作。
- **学习重点**：`I + 动词`、`at + 时间` 和动作顺序。
- **完成标准**：能用三至四句介绍自己的真实早晨。
- **对话场景**：同学比较每天几点到学校。
- **人物**：Lin；同学 Alex。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Alex: What time do you get up?
  2. Lin: I get up at seven.
  3. Alex: Do you have breakfast at home?
  4. Lin: Yes. I leave home at eight.
  5. Alex: What do you have for breakfast?
  6. Lin: I have bread and milk.
  7. Alex: Do you drink coffee in the morning?
  8. Lin: No, I don't. I drink tea.
  9. Alex: Do you leave home early every day?
  10. Lin: Yes. I start work at nine.

#### Topic 11 · 白天

- **教学说明**：学习描述上班或上学地点、交通方式以及午餐时间。
- **学习重点**：`go to... by...`、`work/study in/at...` 和时间表达。
- **完成标准**：能说清自己白天在哪里、如何到达以及几点吃午饭。
- **对话场景**：两位新朋友聊平日生活。
- **人物**：Lin；朋友 Mia。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Mia: Do you work or study?
  2. Lin: I work in an office.
  3. Mia: How do you go to work?
  4. Lin: I go to work by bus.
  5. Mia: What time do you have lunch?
  6. Lin: At twelve.
  7. Mia: Do you eat lunch at the office?
  8. Lin: Yes, I do.
  9. Mia: What time do you finish work?
  10. Lin: I finish at five and go home by bus.

#### Topic 12 · 晚上与周末

- **教学说明**：学习描述下班后的活动和周末习惯，并使用 `don't` 表达不做某事。
- **学习重点**：`after work / at night / on weekends` 与一般动词否定。
- **完成标准**：能用四句对比工作日晚上和周末。
- **对话场景**：周五下班前聊天。
- **人物**：Lin；朋友 Ben。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Ben: What do you do after work?
  2. Lin: I go home and relax.
  3. Ben: Do you work on weekends?
  4. Lin: No, I don't. I visit friends on Saturday.
  5. Ben: What do you do at night?
  6. Lin: I usually read or watch TV.
  7. Ben: Do you get up early on Sunday?
  8. Lin: No, I don't.
  9. Lin: I have breakfast late and visit my family.
  10. Ben: That sounds nice.

### 🅴 日常用品与购物

#### Topic 13 · 我有什么

- **教学说明**：学习表达拥有、询问对方有没有，并礼貌借用常见用品。
- **学习重点**：`I have... / Do you have...? / Can I use/borrow...?`。
- **完成标准**：能完成一次“询问—确认—借用—感谢”的交流。
- **对话场景**：共享学习空间内手机没电。
- **人物**：Lin；同桌 Sofia。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Lin: Do you have a charger?
  2. Sofia: Yes, I do.
  3. Lin: Can I use your charger?
  4. Sofia: Sure.
  5. Lin: Thanks.
  6. Sofia: Do you need it now?
  7. Lin: Yes. I need it now.
  8. Sofia: No problem. The charger is in the drawer.
  9. Lin: Can I borrow it for ten minutes?
  10. Sofia: Of course. Just give it back after work.

#### Topic 14 · 询问商品

- **教学说明**：学习询问商品价格、颜色和尺码，并请求试穿。
- **学习重点**：`How much...? / Do you have this in...? / Can I try this on?`。
- **完成标准**：能向店员获取三项商品信息。
- **对话场景**：服装店内挑选衬衫。
- **人物**：顾客 Lin；店员 Mia。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Lin: Excuse me. How much is this?
  2. Mia: It's eighty yuan.
  3. Lin: Do you have this in blue?
  4. Mia: Yes, we do.
  5. Lin: Can I try it on?
  6. Mia: Of course. What size do you need?
  7. Lin: I need a medium.
  8. Mia: Here is a blue one in medium.
  9. Lin: Thank you. Where can I try it on?
  10. Mia: The fitting room is over there.

#### Topic 15 · 选择与决定

- **教学说明**：学习评价商品是否过大、过小或太贵，询问替代品并作出购买决定。
- **学习重点**：`too + 形容词`、`a larger/smaller one`、`I'll take it`。
- **完成标准**：能说明一件商品不合适的原因，并最终选择一件。
- **对话场景**：服装店试衣后作决定。
- **人物**：顾客 Lin；店员 Mia。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Lin: This is too small.
  2. Mia: Do you need a larger one?
  3. Lin: Yes, please.
  4. Mia: How about this one?
  5. Lin: I like it. I'll take it.
  6. Mia: Great. Do you need anything else?
  7. Lin: How much is this jacket?
  8. Mia: It's two hundred yuan.
  9. Lin: That's too expensive for me.
  10. Mia: No problem. Let's take the shirt to the counter.

### 🅵 联系与约见

#### Topic 16 · 打电话与发消息

- **教学说明**：学习在现在不方便通话时说明情况，并请求稍后联系或发送信息。
- **学习重点**：`call you back / send me a message / right now`。
- **完成标准**：能用电话或消息完成一次延后沟通。
- **对话场景**：Lin 上课时接到朋友电话。
- **人物**：Lin；朋友 Alex。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Lin: Hi, Alex. I'm busy right now.
  2. Alex: No problem.
  3. Lin: Can I call you back?
  4. Alex: Sure. Please send me a message.
  5. Lin: Okay. Do you need the address now?
  6. Alex: Yes, please send me the address.
  7. Lin: I have it here.
  8. Alex: Great. Can you send me a photo too?
  9. Lin: Sure. I'll message you after work.
  10. Alex: Thanks. Talk to you then.

#### Topic 17 · 确认时间地点

- **教学说明**：学习询问对方是否有空、提出见面并再次确认时间和地点。
- **学习重点**：`Are you free...? / Do you want to get together...? / Let's get together... / right?`。
- **完成标准**：能和对方商定一个明确的见面时间与地点。
- **对话场景**：朋友通过电话约周末见面。
- **人物**：Lin；朋友 Sofia。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Lin: Are you free tonight?
  2. Sofia: Yes, I am.
  3. Lin: Do you want to get together for coffee?
  4. Sofia: Sure. Let's get together at six.
  5. Lin: Six at the café, right?
  6. Sofia: Right.
  7. Lin: Which café do you mean?
  8. Sofia: The café next to the station.
  9. Lin: Okay. Do you have the address?
  10. Sofia: Yes. I'll send it to you.

#### Topic 18 · 在路上与迟到

- **教学说明**：学习告诉对方自己正在路上、可能迟到、需要对方等待或已经到达。
- **学习重点**：`on my way / running late / Please wait for me / arrive in...`，这些按完整场景句块练习，不重新教学通用的 `I'm + 补充信息` 结构。
- **完成标准**：能在约见出现变化时及时更新自己的状态和位置。
- **对话场景**：约定时间已到，Lin 还在路上。
- **人物**：Lin；正在咖啡店等待的 Sofia。
- **对话 List**：
  1. Sofia: Hi, Lin. Where are you?
  2. Lin: I'm on my way.
  3. Lin: Sorry, I'm running ten minutes late.
  4. Sofia: That's okay.
  5. Lin: Please wait for me. I'm almost there.
  6. Sofia: Are you near the station?
  7. Lin: Yes, but the traffic is slow.
  8. Sofia: I'm inside the café by the window.
  9. Lin: Okay. I'll arrive(be there) in five minutes.
  10. Sofia: No problem. I'll wait here.

## 附：一般现在时速查卡

```text
肯定：
I like coffee.
You need help.
We work during the day.

否定：
I don't like coffee.
You don't need help.
We don't work on Sunday.

疑问：
Do you like coffee?
Do you need help?
What do you do after work?

简短回答：
Yes, I do.
No, I don't.
```

## 附：want / need / would like 速查卡

```text
want + 名词       I want a coffee.
want to + 动词    I want to go home.

need + 名词       I need some help.
need to + 动词    I need to leave.

would like + 名词     I'd like a coffee, please.
would like to + 动词  I'd like to order.

语气：
want = 直接表达想要
need = 表达实际需要
would like = 更礼貌地表达选择或意愿
```

## 附：可数与不可数名词速查卡

```text
可数：
a ticket / two tickets
a bowl / three bowls
Do you have a charger?

不可数：
water / coffee / rice / money / time
I need some water.
Do you have any time?

容器表达：
a bowl of soup
a glass of water
a bowl of rice
```

  ],
  "extension": [
    "I need a charger.",
    "I need an umbrella.",
    "I need some money.",
    "I need to sleep."
  ],
  "contrast": [
    "I want a coffee, but I need some water."
  ]

## 设计检查清单

```text
□ 每个话题对应一个真实高频的生活交流任务
□ like / want / need / would like 在不同生活场景中反复使用并逐步加深
□ 句块自然、口语常见，不为展示语法制造生硬例句
□ 每个核心词至少进入一个核心句块和一个句型示例
□ 每个扩展词都进入 warmup_pipeline
□ 一般动词输出以 I / you / we 为主
□ 场景之间承担不同交际任务，不重复讲同一个话题
□ 18 个 Topic 保留足够练习深度，不继续为了数量拆分
□ 结课任务覆盖个人日常、礼貌选择、购物和联系约见
```

### 常见设计错误

| 错误类型 | 问题 | 调整原则 |
|:---------|:-----|:---------|
| 喜好、需求分散到所有 Topic | 学习者感到一直在重复 | 前段集中建立，后段放进不同任务中应用 |
| 场景词很多但句块很少 | 背了词却不能交流 | 先确定高频句块，再分配词汇 |
| 核心词没有进入输出 | 词表和课程脱节 | 核心词必须进入 chunks 和 patterns |
| 扩展词只列不练 | warmup 无法承担扩展作用 | 每个扩展词必须有替换题 |
| 提前混入第三人称变化 | 第二包主线不清晰 | 本包稳定 I / you / we 的一般动词 |

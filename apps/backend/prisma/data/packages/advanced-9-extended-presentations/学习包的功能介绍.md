# Advanced ⑨ · 长篇展示、答辩与即兴回应（L5）

> 从结构化小型展示，升级为面向不同听众完成 6—10 分钟专业表达和深度问答。

## 学习包概览

| 维度 | 设计 |
|:-----|:-----|
| CEFR | C1 门槛 |
| 前置要求 | Advanced ①—⑧ |
| 核心目标 | 综合信息、控制节奏、回应质疑并即时重组内容 |
| 结课任务 | 完成一场 6—10 分钟展示、答辩和会后摘要 |

## 场景与 Topics

| 场景 | Topics | 交际成果 |

| 论点设计 | 01 明确核心信息；02 分析听众；03 选择证据；04 设计论证路径 | 建立展示骨架 |

| 深度问答 | 13 拆解复合问题；14 回应质疑；15 承认未知；16 连接回主论点 | 稳定处理压力 |

## 逐 Topic 完整教学设计

> 下列契约用于生成 Advanced CSV、Warmup 与 Ink。每个 Topic 都要求多来源或多立场处理、精确语域、反馈后修订，并复用：Course 9 与 Advanced 1、3、6—8 的展示、精确表达、证据和语域。

### Topic 01 · 明确核心信息

- **教学说明**：在“the single claim the audience should remember”中学习明确核心信息；需要综合多个来源或立场，并显式说明限制。
- **核心词（Vocabulary）**：`thesis`, `audience`, `evidence`, `sequence`。
- **扩展词（Extension）**：`signposting`, `rebuttal`。
- **核心句块（Chunks）**：`The central claim I want to establish is that access alone is insufficient.`；`Let me restate the technical point in more practical terms.`；`Let me separate that into two questions before I respond.`；`That challenge changes the scope, but not the central finding.`。
- **句型（Patterns）**：`The central claim is that ___.`；`Let me restate that in ___.`。
- **完成标准**：完成 16—24 轮复杂互动，综合至少两项信息，回应一个实质异议，主动改写并形成有限定的结论。
- **口语输出**：围绕“the single claim the audience should remember”完成 6—10 分钟表达或答辩，处理至少 3 个不可预先知道的追问。
- **微写作**：写 300—500 词展示稿、答辩记录与修订说明，控制结构、语域、证据归属和结论强度；不得照抄口语稿。
- **反馈与重做**：依据任务、结构、证据、精确度、语域、语法、发音/拼写反馈；完成第二版，7—14 天后更换受众、材料或立场复测。
- **跨包复习**：Course 9 与 Advanced 1、3、6—8 的展示、精确表达、证据和语域；本 Topic 的新表达须在本包后续至少两个 Topic 再次主动调用。
- **具体对话**：
  1. Panel Chair: What is the central claim about the single claim the audience should remember?
  2. Lin: The central claim I want to establish is that access alone is insufficient.
  3. Panel Chair: How will you structure the evidence?
  4. Lin: I'll develop that claim through evidence, mechanism and implication.
  5. Panel Chair: Please explain the technical point more simply.
  6. Lin: Let me restate the technical point in more practical terms.
  7. Panel Chair: What qualification must be added?
  8. Lin: This argument needs to be qualified in two important ways.
  9. Panel Chair: I have two questions about method and impact.
  10. Lin: Let me separate that into two questions before I respond.
  11. Panel Chair: Can you answer them conclusively?
  12. Lin: I cannot answer that conclusively with the available data.
  13. Panel Chair: What uncertainty still needs to be communicated?
  14. Lin: That challenge changes the scope, but not the central finding.
  15. Panel Chair: Give us your final, carefully limited conclusion.
  16. Lin: The revised recommendation is narrower and more defensible.

## 核心语言系统

`The central claim I want to establish is...` / `This needs to be qualified in two ways.` / `Let me separate that into two questions.` / `I can't answer that conclusively, but...`

## 章节与剧集

输出时长从 4 分钟逐步增长到 10 分钟；每章必须包含打断、追问或听众需求变化。

## 数据目标

5 Scenes / 20 Topics / 约 90 Chunks / 约 50 Patterns / 150—180 Vocabulary / 10 Episodes / 20 Ink scripts。

## 设计边界

- 不要求模仿母语者口音。
- 不允许全文背诵作为唯一完成方式。
- 评价结构、准确度、适配、问答和修订能力。

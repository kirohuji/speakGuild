### Topic 01 · 识别需求

- **教学说明**：在"the needs of a neighbourhood group"情境中学习识别需求。本Topic聚焦：Understanding what the community actually needs。
- **核心词（Vocabulary）**：`residents`，`community`，`suggests`，`concerns`。

- **扩展词（Extension）**：`talk`，`shaped`；补充词：`top`。

- **核心句块（Chunks）**：`We first need to understand what residents actually need, not what we assume they need.`；`The available information suggests that transport and safety are the top concerns.`；`Let us talk to people directly before we design any solution.`；`The project should be shaped by the community, not just by us.`。
- **句型（Patterns）**：`We first need to understand ___.`；`Let us ___ before we ___.`。
- **完成标准**：能在陌生变体中完成 12—16 轮互动，使用至少 3 个核心句块和 1 个主句型，并形成结论、决定或下一步。
- **口语输出**：围绕"the needs of a neighbourhood group"完成 2—5 分钟未准备任务；不得照读对话。
- **微写作**：写 120—250 词，内容必须重新组织，不得抄写口语稿。
- **反馈与重做**：按任务完成、结构、词汇、语法及发音/拼写反馈；完成第二次重说和重写，7 天后换情境复测。

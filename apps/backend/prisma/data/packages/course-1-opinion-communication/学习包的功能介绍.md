### 本包新语法

> Foundation 已封版。以下中级语法通过本包的交际场景首次系统引入。

| 语法点 | 本包中的场景应用 | 核心句式 |
|:-------|:----------------|:---------|
| **强调助动词 do / does / did** | 表达观点时加强语气、纠正误解 | `I **do** think it's worth trying.` / `She **did** finish on time.` |

### Topic 01 · 询问看法

- **教学说明**：在"the new class schedule"情境中学习询问看法。本Topic聚焦：A neutral survey of opinions before taking a position。
- **核心词（Vocabulary）**：`timetable`，`advantages`，`views`，`collecting`。

- **扩展词（Extension）**：`worthwhile`，`fair`；补充词：`neutral`，`firsthand`。

- **核心句块（Chunks）**：`From my point of view, the new schedule makes a lot of sense.`；`I see several advantages worth considering.`；`What does everyone else think about this?`；`It seems practical, but I would like to hear other views first.`。
- **句型（Patterns）**：`From my point of view, ___.`；`What does everyone else think about ___?`。
- **完成标准**：能在陌生变体中完成 12—16 轮互动，主动使用至少 3 个核心句块、1 个主句型，并完成追问、澄清或确认。
- **口语输出**：围绕"the new class schedule"完成 2—5 分钟未准备任务；不得照读对话。
- **微写作**：写 120—250 词，把同一能力迁移为观点段落或讨论总结。
- **反馈与重做**：第一次输出后按任务完成、连贯、词汇、语法及发音/拼写反馈；不看完整范文完成第二次重说和重写，7 天后换情境复测。
- **跨包复习**：Foundation 8—10 的连接、比较和从句；本 Topic 新表达须在本包后续至少两个 Topic 再次主动调用。



- **词汇扩展（Vocabulary Expansion）**：
  - `thereby` (adv) — She streamlined the workflow, thereby reducing errors significantly.
  - `meanwhile` (adv) — Meanwhile, other departments reported similar challenges.
  - `otherwise` (adv) — We must act now; otherwise, the opportunity will vanish.
  - `regardless` (adv) — Regardless of the outcome, we gained valuable experience.
  - `essentially` (adv) — The two proposals are essentially the same in core strategy.
  - `fundamentally` (adv) — The approach is fundamentally flawed and needs rethinking.
  - `undoubtedly` (adv) — The new policy will undoubtedly affect team morale.
  - `notably` (adv) — Notably, three departments exceeded their quarterly targets.
  - `speculative` (adj) — The article was largely speculative, with few concrete facts.
  - `tentative` (adj) — We have reached a tentative agreement, pending final approval.
  - `conclusive` (adj) — The evidence was not conclusive enough to justify action.

- **具体对话**：
  1. Emma: We need to decide on the new class schedule. Lin, what is your first impression?
  2. Lin: From my point of view, the new schedule makes a lot of sense.
  3. Emma: Can you tell us more about why you think so?
  4. Lin: I see several advantages worth considering. But I would like to hear other views first.
  5. Mia: I have not looked at it closely yet. Could you point out the main change?
  6. Lin: The main change is that classes now start an hour later.
  7. Emma: That could affect students who have morning commitments.
  8. Lin: That is a fair point. What does everyone else think about this?
  9. Ben: I think we should ask more students before deciding.
  10. Lin: That is a good idea. Let us gather more opinions first.

  11. Emma: That is a whereas point. Your nonetheless makes the argument stronger.

# 基础⑤ · 现在时、频率与状态动词 · Present-Time Grammar（L1~L2）

## 学习包概览

本学习包是 **基础系列（Foundation）· 第5包**。前四包帮助学习者完成基础寒暄、日常生活、日常工作和高频动词短语；本包专注于描述习惯、此刻、状态与变化所需的基础语法：

- 一般现在时与现在进行时；
- 习惯、此刻与临时情况的区别；
- 频率副词的位置；
- 状态动词与动作动词；
- `have` 表示拥有与活动；
- `get` 表示到达、收到和变得。

这些语法通过学习、工作和日常生活场景训练，让学习者能准确说明“平时怎样、现在正在做什么、有什么、发生了什么变化”。

> 🎯 **核心目标**：掌握80个场景化语法核心表达（16 Topic × 5），正确使用一般现在时、现在进行时、频率副词、状态动词、`have` 和 `get`。

> ✅ **结课能力**：能够准确描述习惯、频率、眼前正在发生的事、临时变化，以及拥有、活动、到达、收到和变得等常见含义。

> 表达观点、给理由、同意与不同意、澄清和比较选择已拆分至[表达观点学习包的功能介绍.md](表达观点学习包的功能介绍.md)。

## 设计哲学

### 🕐 语法必须放进真实时间语境

每个Topic必须明确说明时间线索、结构和意义：

```text
习惯或常态 → 一般现在时
此刻或临时情况 → 现在进行时
状态意义 → 通常不用进行时
```

### 📐 每个核心表达必须真正进入训练

| 教学资产 | 硬性要求 |
|:---------|:---------|
| 核心表达 | 进入2个完整句块、1个句型和所属Topic对话 |
| 扩展词/表达 | 进入Warmup的辨义、构句和情境回应 |
| 对话 | 连续10句，不使用互不相关的句子凑数量 |
| 语法 | 必须在场景中表达明确的时间或状态意义 |

### 🔗 新鲜感与跨包去重

基础⑤不以“新的语法术语”凑量，而以**新的场景任务 + 新增内容词 + 未登记过的完整句块**扩充资产。允许为语法操练复习 `I / you / he / she / be / do` 等功能词；这些复习词不计入新增核心词。

| 检查对象 | 去重规则 |
|:---------|:---------|
| 场景任务 | 16个Topic不能重复“同一地点 + 同一目标”；例如通勤、前台、共享厨房、线上会议、周末市集各只承担一次主任务 |
| 核心词 | 与基础①—④的 `scene_vocabulary.csv` 比对；已学词标 `review`，不得再计为 `new` |
| 核心句块 | 以完整英文句登记去重；同一语法结构必须换人物、时间线索、动作和任务 |
| 句型 | 可复用语法骨架，但替换槽必须服务新的场景目标 |
| 动词短语 | 基础④已正式教过的完整短语只作复习，不占基础⑤核心名额 |

### 核心句块与句型的数量标准

```text
16 Topic × 5个核心表达 × 2个独立完整句块 = 160个核心句块
16 Topic × 5个核心表达 × 1个可替换句型     = 80个句型模板
16 Topic × 2个扩展词 × 3道主动输出题       = 96道扩展输出题
```

核心句块必须是可直接说出的完整话语，不能把同一句只替换一个单词重复登记。例如，Topic 03 可以登记：

```text
I am taking notes for the afternoon class.
I am waiting for a quiet desk near the window.
```

而 `I am reading.` / `I am writing.` 这类仅换动词的短句只作为替换练习，不重复计入核心句块。

### 🅰️ 频率与现在进行时

#### Topic 01 · 用频率支持观点

- **教学说明**：用行为发生的频率说明一个评价不是偶然印象。
- **学习重点**：先掌握 `always / usually / often / sometimes / rarely / never` 的频率高低；本Topic先练含义，Topic 02再系统练位置和问句。
- **核心单词**：`always, usually, often, never`
- **核心表达**：`I always... / I usually... / I often... / ...is never...`
- **扩展词**：`rarely, sometimes`
- **完成标准**：能把6个频率副词按高低排序，并根据自己的真实习惯说出4个不同频率的句子。
- **场景与人物**：评价一项学习习惯；Emma和Lin。

##### 语法讲解：频率副词表达“发生得多频繁”

频率副词回答的不是“什么时候发生”，而是“一段时间内发生得有多经常”：

| 频率 | 副词 | 中文理解 | 示例 |
|:----:|:-----|:---------|:-----|
| 100% | `always` | 总是 | `I always review after class.` |
| 80%—90% | `usually` | 通常 | `I usually practice in the morning.` |
| 60%—70% | `often` | 经常 | `I often speak with a partner.` |
| 30%—50% | `sometimes` | 有时 | `I sometimes study at night.` |
| 5%—10% | `rarely` | 很少 | `I rarely miss a class.` |
| 0% | `never` | 从不 | `I never skip practice.` |

`never`本身已经是否定意义，不能再加`don't`：

```text
I never miss class.         ✅
I don't never miss class.   ❌
```

##### 本Topic教学流程

1. **建立频率刻度**：先把`always / usually / often / sometimes / rarely / never`放到0%—100%的频率线上。
2. **理解真实含义**：根据“每天、每周多次、偶尔、一次也没有”等情境选择副词，而不是先背中文翻译。
3. **完成正误判断**：重点纠正`don't never`这类双重否定。
4. **进入观点输出**：用真实学习习惯完成“I ___，所以我认为这个习惯有效/无效”的表达。

| 核心表达 | 句块A | 句块B | 句型 |
|:---------|:------|:------|:-----|
| `I always...` | **I always review after class.** | **I always write down new phrases.** | `I always ___.` |
| `I usually...` | **I usually practice in the morning.** | **I usually study for twenty minutes.** | `I usually ___ at/in ___.` |
| `I often...` | **I often speak with a partner.** | **I often use short dialogues.** | `I often ___.` |
| `...is never...` | **The practice is never boring.** | **Emma is never late for class.** | `___ is never ___.` |

- **对话 List**：
  1. Emma: Do you think your study routine works?
  2. Lin: Yes. I always review after class.
  3. Emma: When do you usually practice?
  4. Lin: I usually practice in the morning.
  5. Emma: Do you speak English with anyone?
  6. Lin: I often speak with a partner.
  7. Emma: Is the same practice sometimes boring?
  8. Lin: No. The practice is never boring because the topics change.
  9. Emma: Do you rarely miss a day?
  10. Lin: Yes. That is why I think the routine works.

#### Topic 02 · 频率副词的位置与频率问句

- **教学说明**：学习频率副词在一般动词和`be`动词句中的不同位置，并询问一项行为发生得多频繁。
- **学习重点**：`I usually practice` 中副词放在一般动词前；`Emma is always ready` 中副词放在`be`后；问句使用 `How often do you...?`。
- **核心单词**：`once, twice, late, notes`
- **核心表达**：`How often do you...? / I...once/twice a... / ...is always... / ...is sometimes...`
- **扩展词**：`regularly, seldom`
- **完成标准**：能正确放置6个频率副词，并完成两组“询问频率—给出次数—表达观点”的对话。
- **场景与人物**：讨论学习和会议频率；Emma和Lin。

##### 语法讲解：频率副词放在哪里？

先判断句子的主要动词：

| 句子类型 | 位置规则 | 示例 |
|:---------|:---------|:-----|
| 一般动词 | 频率副词放在动词前 | `I usually practice in the morning.` |
| `be`动词 | 频率副词放在`am/is/are`后 | `Emma is always ready.` |
| 含否定 | 放在主要动词前 | `I don't usually study at night.` |

```text
I practice usually in the morning.   ❌
I usually practice in the morning.   ✅

Emma always is ready.                 ❌
Emma is always ready.                 ✅
```

询问频率使用：

```text
How often + do/does + 主语 + 动词原形?
How often do you practice?
How often does Ben study?
```

回答次数时使用：

```text
once a day
twice a week
three times a month
```

##### 本Topic教学流程

1. **识别动词类型**：先判断句中是一般动词还是`be`动词。
2. **放置频率副词**：一般动词前、`be`动词后；完成错误句排序。
3. **学习频率问答**：把陈述句改成`How often do/does...?`，再用`once/twice/three times`回答。
4. **使用频率作依据**：先报告频率，再说明自己对课程、习惯或安排的看法。

| 核心表达 | 句块A | 句块B | 句型 |
|:---------|:------|:------|:-----|
| `How often do you...?` | **How often do you practice speaking?** | **How often do you review your notes?** | `How often do you ___?` |
| `I...once/twice a...` | **I practice speaking twice a week.** | **I review my notes once a day.** | `I ___ once/twice a ___.` |
| `...is always...` | **Emma is always ready for class.** | **The weekly meeting is always useful.** | `___ is always ___.` |
| `...is sometimes...` | **The bus is sometimes late.** | **The afternoon class is sometimes noisy.** | `___ is sometimes ___.` |

- **对话 List**：
  1. Emma: How often do you practice speaking?
  2. Lin: I practice speaking twice a week.
  3. Emma: How often do you review your notes?
  4. Lin: I review them once a day.
  5. Emma: Is your practice time regular?
  6. Lin: Yes. My morning practice is always quiet.
  7. Emma: What about the afternoon class?
  8. Lin: The afternoon class is sometimes noisy.
  9. Emma: Do you seldom miss your practice?
  10. Lin: Yes. Regular practice works well for me.

#### Topic 03 · 现在进行时肯定句

- **教学说明**：系统学习用现在进行时描述自己、他人、多人和天气此刻正在发生的动作。
- **学习重点**：结构必须完整：`I am / he is / they are + 动词-ing`；`-ing`不能单独充当谓语。
- **核心单词**：`do, read, wait, rain`
- **核心表达**：`I am...ing / He/She is...ing / We/They are...ing / It is...ing`
- **扩展词**：`happening, right now`
- **完成标准**：能根据四种主语正确选择`am/is/are`，并用4句描述同一幅正在发生的场景。
- **场景与人物**：观察学习中心此刻的活动；Emma和Lin。

##### 语法讲解：现在进行时肯定句

现在进行时表示“说话这一刻正在进行”或“当前一段时间暂时进行”的动作：

```text
主语 + am/is/are + 动词-ing
```

| 主语 | `be`动词 | 示例 |
|:-----|:---------|:-----|
| `I` | `am` | `I am reading.` |
| `he/she/it`、单数人或物 | `is` | `Mia is writing.` |
| `you/we/they`、复数人或物 | `are` | `They are waiting.` |

动词变成`-ing`的基础规则：

| 原形 | 变化 | 示例 |
|:-----|:-----|:-----|
| 大多数动词 | 直接加`-ing` | `read → reading` |
| 以不发音`e`结尾 | 去`e`加`-ing` | `write → writing` |
| 短元音＋单辅音结尾 | 双写末尾辅音 | `run → running` |
| `ie`结尾 | `ie`变`y`再加`-ing` | `lie → lying` |

不能漏掉`be`，也不能在`be`后使用原形：

```text
They waiting outside.      ❌
They are wait outside.     ❌
They are waiting outside.  ✅
```

##### 本Topic教学流程

1. **选择`be`动词**：根据`I / he/she/it / you/we/they`选择`am / is / are`。
2. **生成`-ing`形式**：分别练习直接加`-ing`、去`e`、双写辅音和`ie → y`。
3. **拼成完整谓语**：强制检查句子是否同时具备`be + 动词-ing`。
4. **描述同一场景**：连续描述自己、另一人、多人和天气正在做什么，再对现场作简单评价。

| 核心表达 | 句块A | 句块B | 句型 |
|:---------|:------|:------|:-----|
| `I am...ing` | **I am reading the activity guide.** | **I am waiting by the front desk.** | `I am ___ing ___.` |
| `He/She is...ing` | **Ben is talking to the teacher.** | **Mia is writing down the time.** | `___ is ___ing ___.` |
| `We/They are...ing` | **We are waiting for the class.** | **They are reading the new guide.** | `___ are ___ing ___.` |
| `It is...ing` | **It is raining outside.** | **The class is starting now.** | `It/The ___ is ___ing ___.` |

- **对话 List**：
  1. Emma: What is happening in the learning center?
  2. Lin: I am reading the activity guide.
  3. Emma: What is Ben doing?
  4. Lin: He is talking to the teacher.
  5. Emma: Where is Mia?
  6. Lin: She is writing down the class time.
  7. Emma: What are the other students doing?
  8. Lin: They are waiting for the class.
  9. Emma: Is the weather changing too?
  10. Lin: Yes. It is raining outside right now.

#### Topic 04 · 现在进行时否定句与疑问句

- **教学说明**：学习说明某人此刻没有做什么，并询问正在进行的动作。
- **学习重点**：否定词`not`放在`am/is/are`后；一般疑问句把`am/is/are`提前，不能使用`do/does`。
- **核心单词**：`call, cook, study, work`
- **核心表达**：`I'm not...ing / ...isn't...ing / Are you...ing? / What is...doing?`
- **扩展词**：`still, outside`
- **完成标准**：能把4个进行时肯定句分别改成否定句和疑问句，并正确回答人物当前状态。
- **场景与人物**：电话中确认同事正在做什么；Ben和Lin。

##### 语法讲解：否定句、一般疑问句和特殊疑问句

否定句把`not`放在`am/is/are`后：

```text
I am working.      → I am not working.
Mia is cooking.    → Mia is not cooking.
They are waiting.  → They are not waiting.
```

一般疑问句把`am/is/are`移到主语前，不使用`do/does`：

```text
You are studying.  → Are you studying?
Ben is working.    → Is Ben working?

Do you are studying?   ❌
Are you studying?      ✅
```

简短回答也要保留`be`动词：

```text
Are you working? — Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
Is Mia cooking? — Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.
```

询问具体动作使用：

```text
What + am/is/are + 主语 + doing?
What is Ben doing?
What are they doing?
```

##### 本Topic教学流程

1. **肯定改否定**：在`am/is/are`后加入`not`，不改变`-ing`形式。
2. **肯定改问句**：把`am/is/are`移动到主语前，明确不能加入`do/does`。
3. **练习简短回答**：使用`Yes, I am / No, I'm not / Yes, she is / No, she isn't`。
4. **完成信息差任务**：通过电话询问不同人物正在做什么、没有做什么，并根据答案判断当前情况。

| 核心表达 | 句块A | 句块B | 句型 |
|:---------|:------|:------|:-----|
| `I'm not...ing` | **I'm not working at the office today.** | **I'm not calling about the meeting.** | `I'm not ___ing ___.` |
| `...isn't...ing` | **Ben isn't working outside.** | **Mia isn't cooking right now.** | `___ isn't ___ing ___.` |
| `Are you...ing?` | **Are you studying right now?** | **Are you calling from home?** | `Are you ___ing ___?` |
| `What is...doing?` | **What is Ben doing now?** | **What is Mia doing outside?** | `What is ___ doing ___?` |

- **对话 List**：
  1. Ben: Are you working at the office today?
  2. Lin: No. I'm not working at the office today.
  3. Ben: Are you studying right now?
  4. Lin: Yes. I am studying at home.
  5. Ben: What is Mia doing?
  6. Lin: She is reading, but she isn't calling anyone.
  7. Ben: Is she cooking lunch?
  8. Lin: No. Mia isn't cooking right now.
  9. Ben: What is Emma doing outside?
  10. Lin: She is waiting for the afternoon class.

### 🅱️ 状态、活动与变化

#### Topic 05 · 区分习惯、此刻和临时变化

- **教学说明**：用双时态对比解释为什么今天的观点可能与平时不同。
- **学习重点**：一般现在时表达习惯；`right now / today / this week / for now`配合现在进行时表达此刻或临时情况。
- **核心单词**：`route, walk, relax, temporary`
- **核心表达**：`I normally..., but right now I'm...ing / Today,...is...ing / This week, I'm...ing / For now, I'm...ing`
- **扩展词**：`at the moment, these days`
- **完成标准**：能根据时间词选择正确时态，并用4组句子对比自己的平时习惯和临时变化。
- **场景与人物**：Lin近期改变通勤方式；Ben和Lin。

##### 语法讲解：一般现在时还是现在进行时？

先判断说话者表达的是“常态”还是“当前情况”：

| 含义 | 常见时间词 | 时态 | 示例 |
|:-----|:-----------|:-----|:-----|
| 习惯、常态、事实 | `usually, every day, often` | 一般现在时 | `I take the bus every day.` |
| 此刻正在发生 | `now, right now, at the moment` | 现在进行时 | `I am walking right now.` |
| 当前阶段的临时安排 | `today, this week, these days, for now` | 现在进行时 | `I am working at home this week.` |

中文只增加“正在、今天、这周”也能表达区别；英语还必须改变动词结构：

```text
I usually work at the office.       习惯
I am working at home today.         今天的临时情况
```

常见错误：

```text
I am work at home today.            ❌
I work at home right now.           ❌（想强调此刻）
I am working at home right now.     ✅
```

##### 本Topic教学流程

1. **识别时间范围**：判断句子描述的是长期习惯、说话此刻，还是当前阶段的临时情况。
2. **匹配时间信号**：把`every day/usually`与一般现在时配对，把`now/today/this week`与现在进行时配对。
3. **完成双向转换**：练习`I usually work...`与`Today I'm working...`之间的结构变化。
4. **解释观点变化**：使用“平时情况—当前例外—我的评价”三步说明为什么今天的看法不同。

| 核心表达 | 句块A | 句块B | 句型 |
|:---------|:------|:------|:-----|
| `I normally..., but right now I'm...ing` | **I normally take the bus, but right now I'm walking.** | **I normally work inside, but right now I'm sitting outside.** | `I normally ___, but right now I'm ___ing.` |
| `Today,...is...ing` | **Today, Ben is working at home.** | **Today, the bus is running slowly.** | `Today, ___ is ___ing ___.` |
| `This week, I'm...ing` | **This week, I'm trying a new route.** | **This week, I'm leaving home earlier.** | `This week, I'm ___ing ___.` |
| `For now, I'm...ing` | **For now, I'm walking to work.** | **For now, I'm using a different route.** | `For now, I'm ___ing ___.` |

- **对话 List**：
  1. Ben: You normally take the bus. Why are you walking?
  2. Lin: I normally take the bus, but right now I'm walking.
  3. Ben: Is the bus late?
  4. Lin: Yes. Today, the bus is running slowly.
  5. Ben: Is this only for today?
  6. Lin: This week, I'm trying a new route.
  7. Ben: How do you feel about the walk?
  8. Lin: At the moment, I think it is relaxing.
  9. Ben: Do you want to keep walking?
  10. Lin: For now, I'm walking, but a bike is another option.

#### Topic 06 · 状态动词与动作动词

- **教学说明**：学习同一个动词在“状态意义”和“动作意义”下的不同用法，并认识不能随意使用进行时的状态动词。
- **学习重点**：`I think`表示观点，`I'm thinking about`表示正在考虑；`look`表示看起来，`look at`表示主动看。
- **核心单词**：`know, believe, remember, belong`
- **核心表达**：`I think... / I'm thinking about... / I feel... / I'm feeling... / It looks... / ...is looking at... / I know...`
- **扩展词**：`seem, matter`
- **完成标准**：能判断8个句子中的动词是状态还是动作，并避免`I'm knowing / I'm believing`等错误。
- **场景与人物**：Lin边观察一项活动边决定是否参加；Emma和Lin。

##### 语法讲解：状态动词为什么通常不用进行时？

状态动词表达的是认知、感受、拥有或关系，不是一个正在展开的可见动作。基础阶段通常使用一般现在时：

```text
know, believe, understand, remember
want, need, like, love
own, belong, seem, matter
```

```text
I know the answer.          ✅
I am knowing the answer.    ❌

This bag belongs to Mia.    ✅
This bag is belonging to Mia. ❌
```

有些动词改变含义后可以使用进行时：

| 一般现在时：状态意义 | 现在进行时：动作意义 |
|:---------------------|:---------------------|
| `I think it is useful.` 我认为 | `I'm thinking about the plan.` 我正在考虑 |
| `It looks good.` 它看起来 | `She is looking at it.` 她正在看 |
| `I feel it is right.` 我觉得 | `I'm feeling tired.` 我此刻感觉 |

判断步骤：

1. 是稳定认知、感受、拥有或关系吗？使用一般现在时。
2. 是此刻主动进行的动作吗？可以使用现在进行时。

##### 本Topic教学流程

1. **识别状态意义**：把认知、感受、需要、拥有和关系类动词归入状态动词。
2. **判断意义是否改变**：对比`think`“认为”和`think about`“考虑”、`look`“看起来”和`look at`“看”。
3. **纠正常见直译**：集中修改`I'm knowing / I'm believing / It is belonging`。
4. **进入观点表达**：用状态动词给出看法，再用动作意义说明自己当前正在考虑或观察什么。

| 核心表达 | 句块A | 句块B | 句型 |
|:---------|:------|:------|:-----|
| `I think... / I'm thinking about...` | **I think the activity is useful.** | **I'm thinking about joining the group.** | `I think ___. / I'm thinking about ___.` |
| `I feel... / I'm feeling...` | **I feel this plan is right.** | **I'm feeling tired right now.** | `I feel ___. / I'm feeling ___.` |
| `It looks... / ...is looking at...` | **It looks interesting to me.** | **Emma is looking at the activity list.** | `It looks ___. / ___ is looking at ___.` |
| `I know...` | **I know the class starts at two.** | **I know this bag belongs to Mia.** | `I know ___.` |

- **对话 List**：
  1. Emma: What do you think about the activity?
  2. Lin: I think the activity is useful.
  3. Emma: Are you ready to join?
  4. Lin: I'm thinking about joining the afternoon group.
  5. Emma: How does the group look?
  6. Lin: It looks friendly to me.
  7. Emma: Why is Mia looking at the list?
  8. Lin: She feels unsure and wants to remember the room number.
  9. Emma: Do you know when the class starts?
  10. Lin: Yes. I know the class starts at two, and I believe it is right for me.

#### Topic 07 · 用have区分状态与活动

- **教学说明**：正确使用 `have` 表示拥有、关系、身体状态和正在进行的活动。
- **学习重点**：拥有、关系和身体状态使用 `have/has`；吃饭、开会和经历某段活动可以使用 `am/is/are having`。
- **核心单词**：`desk, shared, kitchen, window`
- **核心表达**：`I have... / ...has... / I'm having... / We're having...`
- **扩展词**：`own, experience`
- **完成标准**：能判断8个`have`例句是“状态”还是“活动”，并避免`I'm having a car`。
- **场景与人物**：评价新的共享办公室；Mia和Lin。

##### 语法讲解：`have`的状态意义与活动意义

`have`表示拥有、关系、特征或身体状态时，不使用进行时：

```text
I have a car.             拥有
She has two brothers.     关系
He has blue eyes.         特征
I have a headache.        身体状态
```

```text
I'm having a car.         ❌
I'm having two brothers.  ❌
```

`have`与餐食、会议、休息或体验组成活动时，可以使用进行时：

```text
I'm having lunch.
We're having a meeting.
She's having a break.
They're having a good time.
```

判断时不要只看`have`，要看后面的宾语表达“拥有的东西”还是“正在进行的活动”。

##### 本Topic教学流程

1. **观察`have`后的宾语**：把物品、关系、特征、身体状态与餐食、会议、休息、体验分组。
2. **选择时态**：状态意义使用`have/has`；当前活动使用`am/is/are having`。
3. **完成错误辨析**：重点纠正`I'm having a car / She's having blue eyes`。
4. **评价真实场景**：描述一个场所“拥有什么”和人们“正在进行什么活动”，再说明自己的看法。

| 核心表达 | 句块A | 句块B | 句型 |
|:---------|:------|:------|:-----|
| `I have...` | **I have a desk by the window.** | **I have a headache today.** | `I have ___, so I think ___.` |
| `...has...` | **The office has a quiet room.** | **Mia has two meetings today.** | `___ has ___.` |
| `I'm having...` | **I'm having lunch in the shared kitchen.** | **I'm having a short meeting now.** | `I'm having ___ now.` |
| `We're having...` | **We're having a good experience here.** | **We're having coffee with the team.** | `We're having ___ with/in ___.` |

- **对话 List**：
  1. Mia: What do you think about the shared office?
  2. Lin: I think it is comfortable. I have a desk by the window.
  3. Mia: The office has a quiet room too.
  4. Lin: Yes, and I'm having a short meeting there now.
  5. Mia: Do you own the desk?
  6. Lin: No. I use it, but the office owns it.
  7. Mia: How is the overall experience?
  8. Lin: We're having a good experience here.
  9. Mia: Are you having lunch here today?
  10. Lin: Yes. The kitchen is another reason I like this place.

#### Topic 08 · 用get描述到达、收到和变得

- **教学说明**：用 `get` 描述到达、收到和变得，再基于变化给出最终看法。
- **学习重点**：`get + 地点`表示到达，`get + 名词`表示收到，`get + 形容词`表示变得；`am/is/are getting`突出正在变化。
- **核心单词**：`feedback, easy, daily, final`
- **核心表达**：`I get... / I'm getting... / It's getting... / My final view is...`
- **扩展词**：`receive, become`
- **完成标准**：能根据上下文判断`get`的三种含义，并用变化结果给出最终观点。
- **场景与人物**：总结一周的新学习计划；Emma和Lin。

##### 语法讲解：根据`get`后面的内容判断含义

`get`本身不能只记成一个中文意思。先看它后面接什么：

| 结构 | 含义 | 示例 |
|:-----|:-----|:-----|
| `get + home/here/there` | 到达 | `I get home at six.` |
| `get + 名词` | 收到、获得 | `I get useful feedback.` |
| `get + 形容词` | 变得 | `The lesson gets easier.` |
| `be getting + 形容词` | 正在变得 | `I'm getting tired.` |

`get to + 普通地点名词`，但`home/here/there`前通常不加`to`：

```text
I get to the office at eight.  ✅
I get home at six.             ✅
I get to home at six.          ❌
```

变化正在发生时使用现在进行时：

```text
The task gets easier with practice.    一般规律
The task is getting easier now.        正在发生的变化
```

##### 本Topic教学流程

1. **观察`get`后的内容**：地点表示到达，名词表示收到，形容词表示变得。
2. **处理地点结构**：比较`get to the office`与不带`to`的`get home/here/there`。
3. **区分规律与当前变化**：比较`gets easier`与`is getting easier`。
4. **用结果总结观点**：先说明到达时间、收到的信息或正在发生的变化，再给出最终评价。

| 核心表达 | 句块A | 句块B | 句型 |
|:---------|:------|:------|:-----|
| `I get...` | **I get home at six.** | **I get useful feedback every day.** | `I get ___ at/every ___.` |
| `I'm getting...` | **I'm getting better at speaking.** | **I'm getting tired after long lessons.** | `I'm getting ___ at/after ___.` |
| `It's getting...` | **It's getting easier every day.** | **It's getting late, so we need to finish.** | `It's getting ___.` |
| `My final view is...` | **My final view is that the plan works.** | **My final view is that short practice is better.** | `My final view is that ___.` |

- **对话 List**：
  1. Emma: How is your new study plan going?
  2. Lin: I get useful feedback every day.
  3. Emma: Is your speaking changing?
  4. Lin: Yes. I'm getting better at speaking.
  5. Emma: Are the lessons still difficult?
  6. Lin: No. It's getting easier every day.
  7. Emma: What time do you get home after class?
  8. Lin: I get home at six, so the schedule works well.
  9. Emma: What is your final opinion?
  10. Lin: My final view is that short daily practice works.

### 3. 频率副词的位置

```text
一般动词前：I always review after class.
be动词后：Emma is never late.

always → usually → often → sometimes → rarely → never
```

### 4. have与get

| 结构 | 含义 | 示例 |
|:-----|:-----|:-----|
| `have + 物品/关系/状态` | 拥有、具有 | `I have a desk. / I have a headache.` |
| `be having + 活动` | 正在吃、开会或经历 | `We're having a meeting.` |
| `get + 地点` | 到达 | `I get home at six.` |
| `get + 名词` | 收到 | `I get useful feedback.` |
| `get + 形容词` | 变得 | `It is getting easier.` |

## 设计检查清单

```text
□ 每个Topic对应一个真实、清晰的语法使用场景
□ 语法讲解必须明确中文母语者的常见错误和纠正方法
□ 每个核心表达至少进入2个完整句块和1个句型
□ 每个核心词标注 new/review，与基础①—④的 scene_vocabulary.csv 比对去重
□ 每个扩展词都进入 Warmup Pipeline 的辨义、构句和情境回应
□ 频率副词先练含义，再练位置，不一次性塞入所有规则
□ 现在进行时覆盖 I/he/they/it 四种主语和肯定/否定/疑问
□ 状态动词与动作动词的对比至少覆盖 think/know/look/feel
□ have 的状态意义和活动意义明确区分，避免 I'm having a car
□ get 的三种含义（到达/收到/变得）在独立Topic中分别训练
□ 对话连续10句，使用固定人物和连贯任务，不使用互不相关的句子
□ 16个Topic不重复同一地点+同一目标的任务配置
```

### 常见设计错误

| 错误类型 | 问题 | 调整原则 |
|:---------|:-----|:---------|
| 只讲语法规则不讲使用场景 | 学习者知道规则但不会用 | 每个语法点绑定一个真实交流任务 |
| 核心词只出现在词表 | 学习者认识但不会使用 | 必须进入句块和句型 |
| 扩展词没有训练出口 | 词汇分层没有实际意义 | 必须进入 Warmup |
| 一次性塞入过多语法 | 学习者混淆时态和状态 | 按Topic顺序逐步投放 |
| 状态动词过早引入进行时 | 产出 I'm knowing 等错误 | 先稳定一般现在时用法 |

## 输出训练流水线（Warmup Pipeline）

| 阶段 | 训练目标 |
|:-----|:---------|
| `meaning_match` | 识别频率、时态、状态与变化的含义 |
| `chunk_shadowing` | 建立完整语音节奏 |
| `pattern_substitution` | 替换人物、时间、地点与动作 |
| `tense_selection` | 习惯用一般现在；此刻/临时用现在进行 |
| `frequency_placement` | 一般动词前，`be` 后 |
| `state_action_check` | 判断 `think/thinking`、`have/having` 的意义 |
| `have_get_judgment` | 判断拥有、活动、到达、收到与变得 |
| `extension_output` | 每个扩展词至少3道主动输出题 |

### 本包新语法

> Foundation 已封版。以下中级语法通过本包的交际场景首次系统引入。

| 语法点 | 本包中的场景应用 | 核心句式 |
|:-------|:----------------|:---------|
| **第二条件句（虚拟现在）** | 给出委婉建议、表达假设 | `If I **were** you, I **would** apologize.` / `If I **had** more time, I **would** join.` |
| **wish / if only** | 表达遗憾、愿望和礼貌拒绝 | `I **wish** I **could** come.` / `If only I **had known** earlier.` |
| **would（过去习惯）** | 回忆过去的社交场景和习惯 | `My grandmother **would always tell** us stories after dinner.` |

> 以上语法在本包的 Chunks 和 Patterns 中体现，并在后续 Course 5—10 中持续复用。

### Topic 01 · 自然开启话题

- **教学说明**：在"meeting a new neighbour"情境中学习自然开启话题。本Topic聚焦：Starting conversations naturally。
- **核心词（Vocabulary）**：`invitation`,`arrangement`,`support`,`feeling`。

- **具体对话**：
  1. Lin: Hi! I have been meaning to introduce myself. I am Lin, from next door.
  2. Neighbour: Oh, hello! I am Sarah. It is nice to finally meet you.
  3. Lin: Do you mind if I ask how long you have been living here?
  4. Neighbour: About three years now. The neighbourhood has changed a lot.
  5. Lin: That reminds me of something similar—I moved a lot as a kid.
  6. Neighbour: Well, welcome. Let me know if you ever need anything.
  7. Lin: Thank you. That is really kind of you.

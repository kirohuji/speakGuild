### 本包新语法

> Foundation 已封版。以下中级语法通过本包的交际场景首次系统引入。

| 语法点 | 本包中的场景应用 | 核心句式 |
|:-------|:----------------|:---------|
| **第二条件句（虚拟现在）** | 给出委婉建议、表达假设 | `If I **were** you, I **would** apologize.` / `If I **had** more time, I **would** join.` |
| **wish / if only** | 表达遗憾、愿望和礼貌拒绝 | `I **wish** I **could** come.` / `If only I **had known** earlier.` |

### Topic 01 · 自然开启话题

- **教学说明**：在"meeting a new neighbour"情境中学习自然开启话题。本Topic聚焦：Starting conversations naturally。
- **核心词（Vocabulary）**：`reminds`，`similar`，`around`，`next`。

- **扩展词（Extension）**：`mind`，`long`；补充词：`happened`，`meaning`。

- **核心句块（Chunks）**：`That reminds me of something similar that happened to me.`；`I have been meaning to introduce myself—I am Lin, from next door.`；`It is nice to finally meet you. I have seen you around.`；`Do you mind if I ask how long you have been living here?`。
- **句型（Patterns）**：`That reminds me of ___.`；`Do you mind if I ask ___?`。
- **完成标准**：能在陌生变体中完成 12—16 轮互动，使用至少 3 个核心句块和 1 个主句型，并形成结论、决定或下一步。
- **口语输出**：围绕"meeting a new neighbour"完成 2—5 分钟未准备任务；不得照读对话。
- **微写作**：写 120—250 词，内容必须重新组织，不得抄写口语稿。
- **反馈与重做**：按任务完成、结构、词汇、语法及发音/拼写反馈；完成第二次重说和重写，7 天后换情境复测。
- **跨包复习**：Course 1 的观点回应与 Foundation 7—8 的礼貌和连接；本 Topic 新表达须在本包后续至少两个 Topic 再次主动调用。



- **词汇扩展（Vocabulary Expansion）**：
  - `discord` (n) — Growing discord within the leadership team threatened the entire project.
  - `amicable` (adj) — The separation was amicable, with both parties remaining on good terms.
  - `reconciliation` (n) — Both sides have expressed a willingness to work toward reconciliation.
  - `atonement` (n) — He saw the substantial charitable donation as an act of atonement.
  - `amends` (n) — She was determined to make amends for her earlier insensitive remarks.
  - `forgiveness` (n) — True forgiveness requires genuinely letting go of deep-seated resentment.
  - `grievance` (n) — All employees should have a clear, safe process for raising grievances.
  - `remorse` (n) — He expressed genuine remorse for the hurt he had inadvertently caused.
  - `empathy` (n) — The most effective leaders demonstrate genuine empathy toward their teams.
  - `compassion` (n) — She treated every single patient with unwavering compassion and dignity.
  - `reassurance` (n) — Just a few words of sincere reassurance can make an enormous difference.

- **具体对话**：
  1. Lin: Hi! I have been meaning to introduce myself. I am Lin, from next door.
  2. Neighbour: Oh, hello! I am Sarah. It is nice to finally meet you.
  3. Lin: Do you mind if I ask how long you have been living here?
  4. Neighbour: About three years now. The neighbourhood has changed a lot.
  5. Lin: That reminds me of something similar—I moved a lot as a kid.
  6. Neighbour: Well, welcome. Let me know if you ever need anything.
  7. Lin: Thank you. That is really kind of you.

  8. Emma: I value your camaraderie. Our peer means a lot to me.

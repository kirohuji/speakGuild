# 从一个想法走到最终选择

`think` 可以表达观点：`I think it will work.` `think about` 是花时间考虑；`think of` 常表示想到、想出或对某人的看法：`What do you think of it?`

`consider` 比 `think about` 更强调认真权衡，后接名词或 `V-ing`：`consider the cost / moving`，通常不说 `consider to move`。

`decide` 表示得出决定：`decide to leave`、`decide on a date`、`decide between two options`。`choose` 强调从选项中挑选：`choose A over B`、`choose between A and B`、`choose to wait`。

> We thought about the problem, considered taking a taxi, and decided to walk. We chose the river route over the main road.

## 开口任务

在两种周末方案之间选择。先表达初步观点，再认真考虑成本和时间，最后说明决定和选择理由。

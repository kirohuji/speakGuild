### 本包新语法

> Foundation 已封版。以下中级语法通过本包的交际场景首次系统引入。

| 语法点 | 本包中的场景应用 | 核心句式 |
|:-------|:----------------|:---------|
| **被动语态进阶**（现在完成/进行时被动） | 汇报进度、确认状态、描述流程 | `The proposal **has been approved**.` / `Your request **is being reviewed**.` |

### Topic 01 · 理解目标

- **教学说明**：在"the project objective"情境中学习理解目标。本Topic聚焦：Clarifying the goal before starting work。
- **核心词（Vocabulary）**：`prototype`，`summarise`，`timeline`，`success`。

- **扩展词（Extension）**：`outcome`，`clarify`；补充词：`looks`，`month`。

- **核心句块（Chunks）**：`Before I start, I want to make sure I understand what success looks like.`；`The outcome we are aiming for is a working prototype by the end of the month.`；`Could you clarify how this fits into the larger project timeline?`；`Let me summarise the goal to make sure we are aligned.`。
- **句型（Patterns）**：`The outcome we are aiming for is ___.`；`Could you clarify how ___?`。
- **完成标准**：能在陌生变体中完成 12—16 轮互动，使用至少 3 个核心句块和 1 个主句型，并形成结论、决定或下一步。
- **口语输出**：围绕"the project objective"完成 2—5 分钟未准备任务；不得照读对话。
- **微写作**：写 120—250 词，内容必须重新组织，不得抄写口语稿。
- **反馈与重做**：按任务完成、结构、词汇、语法及发音/拼写反馈；完成第二次重说和重写，7 天后换情境复测。
- **跨包复习**：Foundation 3、7、8 与 Course 1 的工作描述、建议和观点互动；本 Topic 新表达须在本包后续至少两个 Topic 再次主动调用。



- **词汇扩展（Vocabulary Expansion）**：
  - `arbitration` (n) — The long-running dispute was finally settled through binding arbitration.
  - `mediation` (n) — Skilled professional mediation helped both parties reach a fair and lasting agreement.
  - `negotiation` (n) — Successful negotiation requires thorough preparation, patience, and empathy.
  - `coalition` (n) — A broad coalition of departments united to support the new initiative.
  - `consortium` (n) — A consortium of leading tech companies is funding the research project.
  - `procurement` (n) — The procurement process for new equipment typically takes four to six weeks.
  - `logistics` (n) — The logistics of coordinating an international conference are enormously complex.
  - `personnel` (n) — All personnel must complete the mandatory safety training by Friday.
  - `roster` (n) — The weekly shift roster is posted on the staff notice board every Friday.
  - `workforce` (n) — The workforce has grown from fifty to over two hundred employees in three years.
  - `viability` (n) — We need to thoroughly assess the commercial viability of this new venture.

- **具体对话**：
  1. Emma: Lin, here is the project brief. Do you have any questions before you start?
  2. Lin: Before I start, I want to make sure I understand what success looks like. The outcome we are aiming for is a working prototype, correct?
  3. Emma: That is right. By the end of the month, we need a functional demo.
  4. Lin: Could you clarify how this fits into the larger project timeline? Is this Phase 1 of a bigger rollout?
  5. Emma: Yes. This prototype will determine whether we get funding for the full version.
  6. Lin: Let me summarise: a working prototype by month-end, which serves as a proof of concept for the full project. Correct?
  7. Emma: Exactly. You have got it.

  8. Lin: That is a accountability approach. The optimisation will help the team.

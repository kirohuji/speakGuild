# 沟通与表达

## 阅读说明

- **及物动词（transitive verb）**：可以直接接宾语。  
  `I need more time.` → `more time` 是宾语。

- **不及物动词（intransitive verb）**：不直接接宾语，可以另接介词短语等成分。  
  `Something happened.` / `Listen to me.`

- **系动词（linking verb）**：接表语，说明主语的身份、性质或状态。  
  `She seems tired.` / `The sky turned dark.`

- **使役结构（causative construction）**：表示使、促使或允许某个动作发生。  
  `make someone do` / `let someone do` / `get someone to do`

- **宾语补足语（object complement）**：在宾语后补充说明宾语的状态、身份或动作。  
  `keep the door open` → `open` 说明门的状态。

- **双宾语结构（double-object construction）**：接两个宾语，通常先说接受者，再说给予的东西。  
  `She gave me a book.` → `me` 是间接宾语，`a book` 是直接宾语。

同一个动词可以有多种用法；以上分类的角度不同，也可以同时适用。

### 结构写法

#### 1. 占位符与符号

| 标记 | 含义 |
| --- | --- |
| `someone / something` | 可替换的人／事物 |
| `noun / adjective` | 名词／形容词 |
| `place / time` | 地点／时间 |
| `clause / wh-clause` | 从句／由 what、why、how 等疑问词引导的从句 |
| `do / doing / done` | 动词原形／V-ing 形式／过去分词 |
| `+` | 连接成分，不写进实际句子 |
| `( )` | 该成分可以省略 |

#### 2. 结构示例

固定的介词、不定式标记等需要保留，占位符换成具体内容。

| 结构 | 实际表达 |
| --- | --- |
| `discuss something with someone` | **discuss the plan with her**<br>和她讨论计划 |
| `reply to + noun` | **reply to an email**<br>回复一封邮件 |
| `advise someone to do` | **advise him to wait**<br>建议他等一等 |
| `admit doing` | **admit making a mistake**<br>承认犯了错误 |
| `say (that) + clause` | **say (that) she is busy**<br>说她在忙；`that` 可以省略 |

# 1. say

## 核心感觉

> **说出某段话、某个信息，侧重说了什么。**

## 语法特点

- **主要用作及物动词**：直接接说的内容、从句或直接引语。
- **接收者用 to 引出**：`say something to someone`，不使用 `say someone something`。
- **被动表达**：`be said to do` 表示据说。

## 高频结构

| 结构 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| **say + something**<br>说某事 | **Did he say anything?**<br>他说什么了吗？ |
| **say that + clause**<br>说…… | **She said that she was tired.**<br>她说她累了。 |
| **say something to someone**<br>对某人说某事 | **I need to say something to you.**<br>我有件事要跟你说。 |
| **say + direct speech**<br>直接引用说的话 | **He said, “I'll call you later.”**<br>他说：“我晚点给你打电话。” |
| **be said to do**<br>据说…… | **He is said to be very wealthy.**<br>据说他很有钱。 |

## 高频搭配

| 搭配 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| **say hello**<br>打招呼 | **Say hello to your parents for me.**<br>替我向你父母问好。 |
| **say sorry**<br>道歉 | **You should say sorry to her.**<br>你应该向她道歉。 |
| **say yes / no**<br>答应 / 拒绝 | **She finally said yes.**<br>她最后答应了。 |
| **say something clearly**<br>清楚地表达某事 | **You need to say it more clearly.**<br>你需要说得更清楚。 |
| **needless to say**<br>不用说 | **Needless to say, everyone was shocked.**<br>不用说，大家都很震惊。 |

## 用法提醒

`She said she was tired` 侧重内容；`She told me she was tired` 还明确指出接收者。

过去式和过去分词都是 `said`，读 /sed/。

## 输出例句

**I didn't know what to say at the time.**  
我当时不知道该说什么。

**She said she would call me later.**  
她说她晚点会给我打电话。

**There's something I've been meaning to say to you.**  
有件事我一直想跟你说。

# 2. tell

## 核心感觉

> **把信息告诉某人，或指示某人做某事。**

## 语法特点

- **及物用法**：传递信息时常接接收者，再接内容或从句。
- **双宾语结构**：`tell someone something` 表示告诉某人某事。
- **指示动作**：`tell someone to do` 表示叫、要求某人做某事。

## 高频结构

| 结构 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| **tell someone something**<br>告诉某人某事 | **Tell me the truth.**<br>告诉我真相。 |
| **tell someone that...**<br>告诉某人…… | **She told me that she was leaving.**<br>她告诉我她要走了。 |
| **tell someone to do**<br>告诉 / 要求某人做 | **He told me to wait outside.**<br>他叫我在外面等。 |
| **tell someone about**<br>告诉某人关于…… | **I'll tell you about it later.**<br>我晚点告诉你这件事。 |
| **tell someone how/what/where...**<br>告诉某人如何 / 什么 / 哪里 | **Can you tell me how this works?**<br>你能告诉我这个怎么用吗？ |

## 高频搭配

| 搭配 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| **tell the truth**<br>说实话 | **To tell the truth, I was nervous.**<br>说实话，我当时很紧张。 |
| **tell a lie**<br>撒谎 | **He admitted that he had told a lie.**<br>他承认自己撒了谎。 |
| **tell a story**<br>讲故事 | **She told us a funny story.**<br>她给我们讲了一个有趣的故事。 |
| **tell the difference**<br>看出区别 | **I can't tell the difference.**<br>我看不出区别。 |
| **you can tell**<br>可以看出来 | **You can tell he's tired.**<br>你可以看出他很累。 |

## 用法提醒

`tell a story / tell the truth / tell a lie` 可以不说接收者。`tell the difference` 表示辨别区别。

否定指示用 `tell someone not to do`。过去式和过去分词都是 `told`。

## 输出例句

**Nobody told me the meeting had been cancelled.**  
没人告诉我会议取消了。

**Can you tell me exactly what happened?**  
你能准确告诉我发生了什么吗？

**My manager told me to take a few days off.**  
我的经理让我休息几天。

# 3. speak

## 核心感觉

> **说话、发言，或使用某种语言。**

## 语法特点

- **不及物用法**：用 `to / with` 引出交谈对象，`about` 引出话题。
- **及物用法**：直接接语言名词，如 `speak English`。
- **场合表达**：`speak at + event` 表示在某场合发言。

## 高频结构

| 结构 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| **speak + language**<br>说某种语言 | **Do you speak Japanese?**<br>你会说日语吗？ |
| **speak to someone**<br>和某人谈话 | **Can I speak to you for a minute?**<br>我能和你聊一分钟吗？ |
| **speak with someone**<br>与某人交流 | **I spoke with my manager yesterday.**<br>我昨天和经理谈过。 |
| **speak about something**<br>谈论某事 | **She spoke about her experience.**<br>她谈到了自己的经历。 |
| **speak at + event**<br>在某场合发言 | **He's speaking at the conference tomorrow.**<br>他明天会在会议上发言。 |

## 高频搭配

| 搭配 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| **speak clearly**<br>清楚地说 | **Please speak clearly.**<br>请说清楚一点。 |
| **speak slowly**<br>慢点说 | **Could you speak a little more slowly?**<br>你能说慢一点吗？ |
| **speak up**<br>大声说；大胆发言 | **You'll need to speak up.**<br>你需要说大声一点。 |
| **speak for someone**<br>代表某人发言 | **I can't speak for everyone.**<br>我不能代表所有人。 |
| **generally speaking**<br>一般来说 | **Generally speaking, the system works well.**<br>一般来说，这个系统运行得不错。 |

## 用法提醒

`speak English` 表示说英语；`talk in English` 表示用英语交谈。

`speak to / with` 都可以用于日常交流；`speak` 并不限于正式发言。过去式 `spoke`，过去分词 `spoken`。

## 输出例句

**I can understand English quite well, but I still need to speak more.**  
我的英语理解得不错，但仍然需要多开口。

**I spoke to him about the problem yesterday.**  
我昨天和他谈了这个问题。

**She speaks very clearly, even when explaining something complicated.**  
即使解释复杂的事情，她也说得很清楚。

# 4. talk

## 核心感觉

> **交谈、谈论，强调交流的过程。**

## 语法特点

- **不及物用法**：交谈对象常用 `to / with`，话题常用 `about`。
- **及物短语结构**：`talk something over / through` 表示商量、谈清楚。
- **说服结构**：`talk someone into / out of doing` 表示劝某人做／不做。

## 高频结构

| 结构 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| **talk to someone**<br>和某人谈话 | **I need to talk to you.**<br>我需要和你谈谈。 |
| **talk with someone**<br>和某人交流 | **I talked with her for an hour.**<br>我和她聊了一个小时。 |
| **talk about something**<br>谈论某事 | **We talked about work.**<br>我们聊了工作的事。 |
| **talk to someone about something**<br>和某人谈某事 | **Have you talked to him about it?**<br>你和他谈过这件事吗？ |
| **talk someone into doing**<br>说服某人做某事 | **She talked me into going.**<br>她把我说服去了。 |

## 高频搭配

| 搭配 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| **talk things through**<br>把问题谈清楚 | **We need to talk things through.**<br>我们需要把事情谈清楚。 |
| **talk it over**<br>商量一下 | **Let's talk it over tonight.**<br>今晚我们商量一下。 |
| **talk for hours**<br>聊上几个小时 | **We talked for hours without noticing the time.**<br>我们聊了几个小时，都没注意时间。 |
| **talk openly**<br>坦诚交谈 | **We need to talk openly about this.**<br>我们需要坦诚谈谈这件事。 |
| **talk someone out of doing**<br>劝某人不要做 | **I tried to talk him out of quitting.**<br>我试着劝他不要辞职。 |

## 用法提醒

一般话题用 `talk about work`；也有直接接名词的固定表达，如 `talk business / talk nonsense`。

`into / out of` 后接 `V-ing`。`small talk` 是名词，表示闲聊。

## 输出例句

**We need to sit down and talk about what happened.**  
我们需要坐下来谈谈到底发生了什么。

**I talked to her about changing the schedule.**  
我和她谈了调整时间表的事情。

**Sometimes talking things through helps me understand what I actually think.**  
有时候把事情说开，反而能帮助我理解自己真正怎么想。

# 5. ask

## 核心感觉

> **提问、索求，或请求某人行动。**

## 语法特点

- **及物用法**：直接接问题或被问的人，也可接疑问从句。
- **双宾语结构**：`ask someone something` 表示问某人某事。
- **请求结构**：`ask someone to do` 表示请求某人做；`ask for something` 表示请求某物。

## 高频结构

| 结构 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| **ask a question**<br>提问 | **Can I ask a question?**<br>我能问个问题吗？ |
| **ask someone something**<br>问某人某事 | **She asked me my name.**<br>她问了我的名字。 |
| **ask someone about something**<br>问某人关于某事 | **I asked him about the project.**<br>我问了他项目的情况。 |
| **ask someone to do**<br>请求某人做某事 | **I asked her to wait.**<br>我请她等一下。 |
| **ask if/whether...**<br>询问是否 | **He asked if I was free.**<br>他问我有没有空。 |

## 高频搭配

| 搭配 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| **ask for help**<br>请求帮助 | **Don't be afraid to ask for help.**<br>不要害怕求助。 |
| **ask for permission**<br>请求许可 | **You need to ask for permission first.**<br>你需要先征得许可。 |
| **ask around**<br>四处打听 | **I'll ask around and see what I can find out.**<br>我会四处问问看。 |
| **ask yourself**<br>问自己 | **Ask yourself what you really want.**<br>问问自己真正想要什么。 |
| **ask too much**<br>要求过多 | **I don't think we're asking too much.**<br>我觉得我们的要求不过分。 |

## 用法提醒

`ask him a question` 是问他一个问题；`ask him for help` 是向他求助；`ask him to help` 是请他帮忙。

间接问句用陈述语序：`She asked where I lived`。

## 输出例句

**I wanted to ask you something about the project.**  
我想问你一个关于项目的问题。

**She asked me to send the file again.**  
她让我再发一次文件。

**If you're unsure, just ask.**  
如果不确定，直接问就行。

# 6. answer

## 核心感觉

> **回答问题、回应某人，或接电话、应门。**

## 语法特点

- **及物用法**：直接接问题、某人、电话等回应对象。
- **不及物用法**：可省略回应对象，如 `He didn’t answer`。
- **内容表达**：可接直接引语或 `that` 从句说明回答内容。

## 高频结构

| 结构 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| **answer a question**<br>回答问题 | **Can you answer this question?**<br>你能回答这个问题吗？ |
| **answer someone**<br>回答某人 | **He didn't answer me.**<br>他没有回答我。 |
| **answer the phone**<br>接电话 | **Can you answer the phone?**<br>你能接一下电话吗？ |
| **answer that...**<br>回答说…… | **She answered that she didn't know.**<br>她回答说她不知道。 |
| **answer + direct speech**<br>用直接引语呈现回答 | **“I don’t know,” she answered.**<br>“我不知道。”她回答道。 |

## 高频搭配

| 搭配 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| **answer correctly**<br>回答正确 | **Only two people answered correctly.**<br>只有两个人答对了。 |
| **answer honestly**<br>如实回答 | **Just answer honestly.**<br>如实回答就行。 |
| **answer immediately**<br>立即回应 | **You don't have to answer immediately.**<br>你不需要马上回答。 |
| **answer the door**<br>应门；去开门 | **Could you answer the door?**<br>你能去开一下门吗？ |
| **answer a message**<br>回复消息 | **I haven't answered his message yet.**<br>我还没回他的消息。 |

## 用法提醒

回答问题说 `answer the question`；名词用法说 `the answer to the question`。

`answer to someone` 是另一种结构，常表示对某人负责；不能据此给“回答问题”加 `to`。

## 输出例句

**I wasn't sure how to answer the question.**  
我当时不知道该怎么回答这个问题。

**He called twice, but I didn't answer.**  
他打了两次电话，但我都没接。

**You don't need to answer right away. Take some time to think.**  
你不用马上回答，可以花点时间想想。

# 7. explain

## 核心感觉

> **把原因、过程或意思说清楚，使人理解。**

## 语法特点

- **及物用法**：直接接解释的内容，也可接 `that / wh-` 从句。
- **对象用 to 引出**：`explain something to someone`，不使用 `explain someone something`。

## 高频结构

| 结构 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| **explain something**<br>解释某事 | **Can you explain the problem?**<br>你能解释一下这个问题吗？ |
| **explain something to someone**<br>向某人解释 | **I explained the situation to her.**<br>我向她解释了情况。 |
| **explain that...**<br>解释说…… | **He explained that he had been delayed.**<br>他解释说自己耽搁了。 |
| **explain why...**<br>解释为什么 | **Can you explain why this happened?**<br>你能解释为什么会这样吗？ |
| **explain how...**<br>解释如何 | **Let me explain how it works.**<br>我来解释一下它是怎么运作的。 |

## 高频搭配

| 搭配 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| **clearly explain**<br>清楚解释 | **She explained it very clearly.**<br>她解释得很清楚。 |
| **difficult to explain**<br>难以解释 | **It's difficult to explain in a few words.**<br>这很难用几句话解释清楚。 |
| **explain yourself**<br>解释自己的行为 | **You'd better explain yourself.**<br>你最好解释一下自己的行为。 |
| **explain in detail**<br>详细解释 | **He explained the process in detail.**<br>他详细解释了这个过程。 |
| **help explain**<br>有助于解释 | **This may help explain the difference.**<br>这或许有助于解释这个差异。 |

## 用法提醒

说 `explain the rule to me`，不用 `explain me the rule`。也可以说 `explain to me how it works`。

`explain yourself` 常是要求对方解释自己的行为或理由。

## 输出例句

**I understand the result, but I can't explain why it happened.**  
我理解这个结果，但我解释不了为什么会发生。

**Let me explain what I meant.**  
让我解释一下我的意思。

**He explained the whole process step by step.**  
他一步一步解释了整个过程。

# 8. describe

## 核心感觉

> **用语言呈现某人、某物或某件事的特征、情况。**

## 语法特点

- **及物动词**：直接接被描述的人、事物或疑问词从句。
- **描述对象与接收者**：`describe something to someone` 表示向某人描述。
- **认定性质**：`describe someone / something as...` 表示形容为。

## 高频结构

| 结构 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| **describe + noun**<br>描述某物 | **Can you describe the man?**<br>你能描述一下那个男人吗？ |
| **describe what happened**<br>描述发生了什么 | **Please describe what happened.**<br>请描述一下发生了什么。 |
| **describe how...**<br>描述如何…… | **She described how the accident happened.**<br>她描述了事故是怎么发生的。 |
| **describe someone / something as...**<br>把A描述为B | **He described the experience as frightening.**<br>他把那次经历形容为很可怕。 |
| **be described as**<br>被描述为 | **The city is often described as peaceful.**<br>这座城市经常被形容为很宁静。 |

## 高频搭配

| 搭配 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| **accurately describe**<br>准确描述 | **It's hard to accurately describe the feeling.**<br>很难准确描述这种感觉。 |
| **briefly describe**<br>简要描述 | **Could you briefly describe your experience?**<br>你能简单描述一下你的经历吗？ |
| **describe in detail**<br>详细描述 | **He described the room in detail.**<br>他详细描述了房间。 |
| **best described as**<br>最适合形容为 | **The atmosphere is best described as relaxed.**<br>这里的氛围最适合形容为轻松。 |
| **difficult to describe**<br>难以描述 | **The sound is difficult to describe.**<br>这种声音很难描述。 |

## 用法提醒

说 `describe the room`，不在宾语前加 `about`。

`describe it as useful` 中的 `as` 需要保留；形容为什么还可用 `as + noun / V-ing`，如 `describe him as a friend / as being helpful`。

## 输出例句

**It's difficult to describe exactly how I felt at the time.**  
很难准确描述我当时是什么感觉。

**Can you describe what you saw?**  
你能描述一下你看到了什么吗？

**She described the place as quiet but slightly unsettling.**  
她把那个地方形容为安静，但又有一点让人不安。

# 9. mention

## 核心感觉

> **简要提到某人某事，或补充一条信息。**

## 语法特点

- **及物动词**：直接接名词、`V-ing` 或从句。
- **接收者用 to 引出**：`mention something to someone` 表示向某人提及。

## 高频结构

| 结构 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| **mention + noun**<br>提到某事 | **Did he mention the meeting?**<br>他提到会议了吗？ |
| **mention that...**<br>提到…… | **She mentioned that she might leave.**<br>她提到自己可能会离开。 |
| **mention doing**<br>提到做某事 | **He mentioned moving abroad.**<br>他提到过搬去国外。 |
| **mention someone by name**<br>点名提到某人 | **She didn't mention him by name.**<br>她没有直接提他的名字。 |
| **not to mention**<br>更不用说 | **It's expensive, not to mention inconvenient.**<br>它很贵，更不用说还不方便。 |

## 高频搭配

| 搭配 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| **briefly mention**<br>简单提到 | **He briefly mentioned the problem.**<br>他简单提了一下这个问题。 |

# 综合对话

**A:** Can I ask you something about the new booking system?

我能问你一个关于新预订系统的问题吗？

**B:** Of course. What happened?

当然。怎么了？

**A:** I reported a problem yesterday, but nobody has replied to my email.

我昨天报告了一个问题，但一直没人回复我的邮件。

**B:** Can you describe what happened?

你能描述一下发生了什么吗？

**A:** The system said my booking had failed, but it still charged me. I explained that in the email.

系统显示预订失败了，却还是扣了款。我在邮件里说明了这一点。

**B:** Did you mention the booking number?

你提预订编号了吗？

**A:** Yes. I also told them when it happened. They claim the payment went through normally.

提了。我还告诉了他们事情发生的时间。他们声称付款正常完成了。

**B:** I’d suggest asking them to check the booking and the payment separately. You could attach a screenshot, too.

我建议请他们分别核查预订和付款，也可以附上一张截图。

**A:** That makes sense. I don’t want to argue with anyone, but I need a clear answer.

有道理。我不想和谁争论，但我需要一个明确的答复。

**B:** I agree. If they still don’t answer, I’d advise you to speak to a manager.

我同意。如果他们还是不回复，我建议你找经理谈。

**A:** Could you help me write the message? I’m not sure how to express my concern politely.

你能帮我写这条消息吗？我不太确定怎样礼貌地表达我的担忧。

**B:** Sure. Start by explaining the problem, then ask what they can do to resolve it.

可以。先说明问题，再问他们能怎样解决。

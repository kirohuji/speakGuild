# 人际关系与影响

## 阅读说明

- **及物动词（transitive verb）**：可以直接接宾语。  
  `I need more time.` → `more time` 是宾语。

- **不及物动词（intransitive verb）**：不直接接宾语，可以另接介词短语等成分。  
  `Something happened.` / `Listen to me.`

- **系动词（linking verb）**：接表语，说明主语的身份、性质或状态。  
  `She seems tired.` / `The sky turned dark.`

- **使役结构（causative construction）**：表示使、促使或允许某个动作发生。  
  `make someone do` / `let someone do` / `get someone to do`

- **宾语补足语（object complement）**：在宾语后补充说明宾语的状态、身份或动作。  
  `keep the door open` → `open` 说明门的状态。

- **双宾语结构（double-object construction）**：接两个宾语，通常先说接受者，再说给予的东西。  
  `She gave me a book.` → `me` 是间接宾语，`a book` 是直接宾语。

同一个动词可以有多种用法；以上分类的角度不同，也可以同时适用。

### 结构写法

#### 1. 占位符与符号

| 标记 | 含义 |
| --- | --- |
| `someone / something` | 可替换的人／事物 |
| `noun / adjective` | 名词／形容词 |
| `place / time` | 地点／时间 |
| `clause / wh-clause` | 从句／由 what、why、how 等疑问词引导的从句 |
| `do / doing / done` | 动词原形／V-ing 形式／过去分词 |
| `+` | 连接成分，不写进实际句子 |
| `( )` | 该成分可以省略 |

#### 2. 结构示例

把占位符换成具体内容，保留固定介词和不定式标记。

| 结构 | 实际表达 |
| --- | --- |
| `persuade someone to do` | **persuade him to stay**<br>说服他留下 |
| `remind someone of something` | **remind me of home**<br>让我想起家 |
| `share something with someone` | **share the news with her**<br>把消息分享给她 |
| `thank someone for doing` | **thank him for waiting**<br>感谢他耐心等候 |
| `convince someone (that) + clause` | **convince me (that) it’s safe**<br>让我相信这很安全，`that` 可以省略 |

# 1. support

## 核心感觉

> **为某人或某事提供帮助、认可或支撑。**

## 语法特点

- **及物动词**：直接接人、决定、项目等，也可接论点或结论，表示证据支持。
- **动作结构**：`support someone in doing` 表示在某人做某事时给予支持。

## 高频结构

| 结构 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| **support someone**<br>支持某人 | **My family supported me throughout the process.**<br>我的家人在整个过程中都支持我。 |
| **support an idea**<br>支持观点 | **I support the idea in principle.**<br>原则上我支持这个想法。 |
| **support someone in doing**<br>支持某人做某事 | **They supported her in starting her own business.**<br>他们支持她创业。 |
| **support someone through something**<br>陪伴并支持某人度过困难 | **She supported him through a difficult period.**<br>她陪他度过了一段艰难时期。 |
| **support a project**<br>支持项目 | **The company agreed to support the project.**<br>公司同意支持这个项目。 |

## 高频搭配

| 搭配 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| **fully support**<br>完全支持 | **I fully support your decision.**<br>我完全支持你的决定。 |
| **financially support**<br>经济支持 | **His parents supported him financially.**<br>他的父母在经济上支持他。 |
| **support a friend**<br>支持朋友 | **She supported a friend who was going through a hard time.**<br>她支持了一位正经历困难的朋友。 |
| **support a family**<br>养家 | **He works two jobs to support his family.**<br>他打两份工来养家。 |
| **support a conclusion**<br>支持结论 | **The data support this conclusion.**<br>数据支持这一结论。 |

## 用法提醒

`support her decision` 是支持她的决定。`support her through a difficult time` 是陪她度过难关。

`technical support` 和 `emotional support` 中的 `support` 是名词。

## 输出例句

**Good feedback should support improvement rather than simply point out mistakes.**  
好的反馈应该支持进步，而不只是指出错误。

**I supported his decision even though I wouldn't have made the same choice.**  
虽然如果是我不会作同样的选择，但我还是支持他的决定。

**Sometimes simply listening is enough to make someone feel supported.**  
有时候，仅仅认真倾听就足以让一个人感到被支持。

# 2. affect

## 核心感觉

> **使某人的感受、某事的状态或结果发生变化。**

## 语法特点

- **及物动词**：直接接受影响的人或事物，也可接 `how` 等疑问词结构。
- **被动结构**：`be affected by` 表示受到某人或某事影响。

## 高频结构

| 结构 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| **affect someone**<br>影响某人 | **The news affected him deeply.**<br>这个消息对他影响很深。 |
| **affect something**<br>影响某事 | **The change may affect performance.**<br>这个改变可能影响性能。 |
| **affect how...**<br>影响……的方式 | **Sleep affects how well you think.**<br>睡眠会影响你的思考状态。 |
| **be affected by**<br>受到……影响 | **The result was affected by the weather.**<br>结果受到了天气影响。 |
| **seriously affect**<br>严重影响 | **The delay seriously affected the schedule.**<br>延误严重影响了进度。 |

## 高频搭配

| 搭配 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| **directly affect**<br>直接影响 | **This directly affects users.**<br>这会直接影响用户。 |
| **negatively affect**<br>产生负面影响 | **Stress can negatively affect sleep.**<br>压力会对睡眠产生负面影响。 |
| **deeply affect**<br>深深影响 | **The experience deeply affected her.**<br>那段经历深深影响了她。 |
| **affect performance**<br>影响表现 | **Lack of sleep affects performance.**<br>睡眠不足会影响表现。 |
| **affect behavior**<br>影响行为 | **Environment affects behavior.**<br>环境会影响行为。 |

## 用法提醒

常见搭配是 `affect sleep` 和 `affect the result`。名词常用 `effect`，如 `have an effect on sleep`。

## 输出例句

**The way people respond to you can affect your confidence.**  
别人回应你的方式会影响你的信心。

**One bad experience shouldn't affect every decision you make afterward.**  
一次糟糕经历不应该影响你之后的每一个决定。

**Small changes in routine can affect how you feel throughout the day.**  
日常习惯中的小变化会影响你一整天的感受。

# 3. influence

## 核心感觉

> **影响某人的想法、选择或事物的发展方向。**

## 语法特点

- **及物动词**：直接接人、决定、行为等，也可接 `how` 从句。
- **被动结构**：`be influenced by` 引出影响来源。

## 高频结构

| 结构 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| **influence someone**<br>影响某人 | **His teacher influenced him greatly.**<br>他的老师对他影响很大。 |
| **influence a decision**<br>影响决定 | **Money influenced the final decision.**<br>金钱影响了最终决定。 |
| **influence how someone thinks**<br>影响思考方式 | **Culture influences how people think.**<br>文化会影响人的思考方式。 |
| **be influenced by**<br>受到……影响 | **We're all influenced by our environment.**<br>我们都会受到环境影响。 |
| **influence what someone does**<br>影响某人的做法 | **Your advice influenced what I did next.**<br>你的建议影响了我接下来的做法。 |

## 高频搭配

| 搭配 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| **strongly influence**<br>强烈影响 | **The experience strongly influenced his later work.**<br>这段经历强烈影响了他后来的工作。 |
| **positively influence**<br>积极影响 | **Good teachers can positively influence students.**<br>好老师能积极影响学生。 |
| **influence consumer choices**<br>影响消费者选择 | **Product reviews can influence consumer choices.**<br>产品评论会影响消费者的选择。 |
| **influence the outcome**<br>左右结果 | **Several factors influenced the outcome.**<br>有几个因素影响了结果。 |
| **be easily influenced**<br>容易受影响 | **He’s easily influenced by what he reads online.**<br>他容易受到网上所读内容的影响。 |

## 用法提醒

`influence a decision` 侧重左右决定。`affect a decision` 也可以表示对决定产生影响，两者有重叠。

`have an influence on` 中的 `influence` 是名词。

## 输出例句

**The people around you can influence the way you think without you noticing it.**  
身边的人可能会在你没有意识到的情况下影响你的思维方式。

**His advice influenced my decision, but it wasn't the only reason.**  
他的建议影响了我的决定，但并不是唯一原因。

**Books can influence how you see the world long after you finish reading them.**  
一本书读完很久以后，仍然可能影响你看待世界的方式。

# 4. persuade

## 核心感觉

> **通过劝说让某人采取行动或接受某种看法。**

## 语法特点

- **及物动词**：直接接被说服的人，后面可接 `to do` 或 `that` 从句。
- **介词结构**：`persuade someone into doing` 表示劝动对方去做，`out of doing` 表示劝对方放弃。

## 高频结构

| 结构 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| **persuade someone to do**<br>说服某人做 | **I persuaded him to stay.**<br>我说服他留下。 |
| **persuade someone not to do**<br>说服某人不要做 | **She persuaded me not to quit.**<br>她说服我不要放弃。 |
| **persuade someone into doing**<br>说动某人做 | **They persuaded him into joining the team.**<br>他们说服他加入团队。 |
| **persuade someone that...**<br>使某人相信 | **I couldn't persuade her that it was safe.**<br>我没能让她相信这是安全的。 |
| **be persuaded to do**<br>被说服去做 | **He was eventually persuaded to apologize.**<br>他最终被说服去道歉。 |

## 高频搭配

| 搭配 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| **successfully persuade**<br>成功说服 | **She successfully persuaded him to reconsider.**<br>她成功说服他重新考虑。 |
| **try to persuade**<br>尝试说服 | **I tried to persuade her to wait.**<br>我试着劝她等等。 |
| **difficult to persuade**<br>很难说服 | **He's difficult to persuade once he's made up his mind.**<br>他一旦下定决心就很难被说服。 |
| **persuade someone otherwise**<br>让某人改变看法 | **Nothing could persuade him otherwise.**<br>没有什么能让他改变看法。 |
| **persuade someone out of doing**<br>劝某人放弃做某事 | **She persuaded him out of quitting on the spot.**<br>她劝住了他，让他没有当场辞职。 |

## 用法提醒

`I persuaded him to stay` 表示劝说成功。`I tried to persuade him to stay` 表示试着劝他留下。

`persuade him not to go` 把 `not` 放在 `to go` 前。

## 输出例句

**She persuaded me to give the idea another chance.**  
她说服我再给这个想法一次机会。

**It's easier to persuade people when they feel understood first.**  
先让人感到被理解，会更容易说服他们。

**I couldn't persuade him to change his mind.**  
我没能说服他改变主意。

# 5. convince

## 核心感觉

> **让某人相信某事或接受某个判断，也可表示说服某人行动。**

## 语法特点

- **及物动词**：直接接被说服的人，可用 `that` 从句或 `of + noun` 补充所相信的内容。
- **行动结构**：`convince someone to do` 表示说服某人做某事。

## 高频结构

| 结构 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| **convince someone that...**<br>使某人相信…… | **I convinced him that the plan could work.**<br>我让他相信这个计划可行。 |
| **convince someone of something**<br>使某人确信某事 | **The evidence convinced me of his innocence.**<br>证据让我相信他是无辜的。 |
| **be convinced that...**<br>确信…… | **I'm convinced that this is the right approach.**<br>我确信这是正确的方法。 |
| **convince someone to do**<br>说服某人做 | **She convinced me to try again.**<br>她说服我再试一次。 |
| **fail to convince**<br>没能说服 | **His explanation failed to convince me.**<br>他的解释没能说服我。 |

## 高频搭配

| 搭配 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| **fully convinced**<br>完全相信 | **I'm not fully convinced yet.**<br>我还没有完全相信。 |
| **hard to convince**<br>很难说服 | **He's hard to convince without evidence.**<br>没有证据很难说服他。 |
| **be enough to convince**<br>足以让人相信 | **The screenshots were enough to convince me.**<br>这些截图足以让我相信。 |
| **almost convince**<br>差点说服 | **You almost convinced me to change my mind.**<br>你差点说服我改变主意。 |
| **convince yourself**<br>说服自己相信 | **I kept trying to convince myself that everything was fine.**<br>我一直试图说服自己一切都没问题。 |

## 用法提醒

`convince me that it’s safe` 侧重让我相信这很安全。`convince me to try it` 侧重说服我尝试。

`I’m convinced` 可以单独用，表示我已经相信了。

## 输出例句

**The data convinced me that my original assumption was wrong.**  
数据让我相信自己最初的假设错了。

**You don't need to convince everyone that your choice is right.**  
你不需要让所有人都相信你的选择是正确的。

**I wasn't convinced until I saw the results myself.**  
直到亲眼看到结果，我才真正相信。

# 6. remind

## 核心感觉

> **使人想起某事，或提醒某人留意、记得行动。**

## 语法特点

- **及物动词**：直接接被提醒的人。
- **介词结构**：`remind someone of something` 常表示使某人想起，`about` 常引出待处理或要留意的事项。
- **行动与事实**：可接 `to do` 或 `that` 从句。

## 高频结构

| 结构 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| **remind someone of something**<br>使某人想起 | **This song reminds me of school.**<br>这首歌让我想起学生时代。 |
| **remind someone about something**<br>提醒某人某件事 | **Remind me about the meeting later.**<br>晚点提醒我开会。 |
| **remind someone to do**<br>提醒某人去做 | **Remind me to call him.**<br>提醒我给他打电话。 |
| **remind someone that...**<br>提醒某人某个事实 | **She reminded me that we had a deadline.**<br>她提醒我我们还有截止日期。 |
| **be reminded of**<br>被勾起回忆 | **I was reminded of something he once said.**<br>我想起了他以前说过的一句话。 |

## 高频搭配

| 搭配 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| **gently remind**<br>委婉提醒 | **I gently reminded him that the deadline was tomorrow.**<br>我委婉地提醒他，明天就到截止日期了。 |
| **constantly remind**<br>不断提醒 | **I have to constantly remind myself to slow down.**<br>我得不断提醒自己慢一点。 |
| **remind yourself**<br>提醒自己 | **Remind yourself why you started.**<br>提醒自己为什么开始。 |
| **need to be reminded**<br>需要有人提醒 | **I sometimes need to be reminded to take a break.**<br>有时我需要别人提醒我休息。 |
| **remind someone why...**<br>让某人想起为什么…… | **The letter reminded me why I’d taken the job.**<br>这封信让我想起当初为什么接受这份工作。 |

## 用法提醒

`This song reminds me of home` 是这首歌让我想起家。`Remind me to call home` 是提醒我给家里打电话。

`reminder` 是名词，如 `set a reminder`。

## 输出例句

**Please remind me to send the file this afternoon.**  
下午提醒我把文件发出去。

**That conversation reminded me why I started the project in the first place.**  
那次谈话让我重新想起自己最初为什么开始这个项目。

**I sometimes need to remind myself that progress doesn't have to be fast.**  
有时候我需要提醒自己，进步不一定要很快。

# 7. invite

| **invite someone to + event**<br>邀请某人参加 | **They invited us to dinner.**<br>他们邀请我们吃晚饭。 |
| **invite someone to do**<br>邀请某人做 | **She invited me to join them.**<br>她邀请我加入他们。 |
| **be invited to**<br>被邀请参加 | **I was invited to the meeting.**<br>我受邀参加会议。 |
| **invite questions**<br>欢迎提问 | **The speaker invited questions at the end.**<br>演讲者最后欢迎大家提问。 |

## 高频搭配

| 搭配 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |

## 用法提醒

**She invited me to join the discussion.**  
她邀请我加入讨论。

**We invited a few users to test the early version.**  
我们邀请了一些用户测试早期版本。

**A good question can invite someone to share more.**  
一个好的问题可以让对方更愿意继续分享。

# 9. contact

## 核心感觉

> **主动与人或机构取得联系。**

## 语法特点

- **及物动词**：直接接联系对象，如 `contact me`。
- **方式结构**：用 `by email / by phone` 等说明联系方式。

## 高频结构

| 结构 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |

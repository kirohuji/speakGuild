# 特别容易混淆的几组

## 1. lie — lay — lain

表示：

> 躺

> lie — lay — lain

例：

> He **lay** on the bed yesterday.

> He has **lain** there for hours.

---

## 2. lay — laid — laid

表示：

> 放置某物

> lay — laid — laid

例：

> She **laid** the book on the table.

---

## 3. lie — lied — lied

表示：

> 说谎

> lie — lied — lied

例：

> He **lied** to me.

---

## 4. rise — rose — risen

表示：

> 自己上升

> Prices **rose** last year.

> Prices have **risen** sharply.

---

## 5. raise — raised — raised

这是规则动词。

表示：

> 提高某物、举起某物

> They **raised** the price.

所以：`rise`（自己上升）与 `raise`（使某物上升）要分开记。

---

## 6. fall — fell — fallen

表示：

> 掉落

> The tree **fell**.

---

## 7. fell — felled — felled

另一个独立动词：

> 砍倒

> They **felled** the tree.

---

## 8. find — found — found

表示：

> 找到

> I **found** my phone.

---

## 9. found — founded — founded

另一个独立动词：

> 创立

> The company was **founded** in 2005.

# 常见英式 / 美式形式

下面这些动词经常同时存在两套形式。

| 动词 | 常见形式 |
|---|---|
| burn | burned / burnt |
| dream | dreamed / dreamt |
| learn | learned / learnt |
| smell | smelled / smelt |
| spell | spelled / spelt |
| spill | spilled / spilt |
| spoil | spoiled / spoilt |
| leap | leaped / leapt |
| lean | leaned / leant |
| kneel | kneeled / knelt |

大体上：**美式英语更常保留 -ed**，例如 `learned`、`dreamed`、`burned`；英式英语里 `learnt`、`dreamt`、`burnt` 也很常见。

两种形式都可能是标准英语。

---

# get 的特殊形式

get 是一个需要单独注意的高频动词。

## 英式英语

> get — got — got

## 美式英语

> get — got — gotten

例如：

> I've **got** a car.  
> 我有一辆车。

> Things have **gotten** better.  
> 情况已经好转了。

美式英语里的 gotten 经常强调：

> 获得  
> 变化  
> 发展

---

# hang 的特殊形式

普通“挂、悬挂”：

> hang — hung — hung

例如：

> She **hung** the picture on the wall.

涉及绞刑时通常：

> hang — hanged — hanged

例如：

> The prisoner was **hanged**.

---

# bear 的特殊过去分词

通常：

> bear — bore — borne

例如：

> The cost was **borne** by the company.

但“出生”固定使用 `born`，例如：

> I was **born** in 2000.

---

# read 的特殊读音

| 形式 | 拼写 | 发音 |
|---|---|---|
| 原形 | read | /riːd/ |
| 过去式 | read | /red/ |
| 过去分词 | read | /red/ |

例：

> I **read** /riːd/ every day.

> I **read** /red/ it yesterday.

> I've **read** /red/ it before.

# 最核心的一批建议优先熟悉

如果只看使用频率，下面这些动词尤其值得做到几乎不需要思考：

| 原形 | 过去式 | 过去分词 |
|---|---|---|
| be | was / were | been |
| have | had | had |
| do | did | done |
| go | went | gone |
| get | got | got / gotten |
| make | made | made |
| take | took | taken |
| come | came | come |
| see | saw | seen |
| know | knew | known |
| think | thought | thought |
| say | said | said |
| tell | told | told |
| give | gave | given |
| find | found | found |
| leave | left | left |
| feel | felt | felt |
| bring | brought | brought |
| keep | kept | kept |
| hold | held | held |
| write | wrote | written |
| read | read | read |
| speak | spoke | spoken |
| meet | met | met |
| become | became | become |
| begin | began | begun |
| run | ran | run |
| eat | ate | eaten |
| drink | drank | drunk |
| sleep | slept | slept |

这些动词已经能够覆盖非常大一部分日常叙述。

例如：

> I **went** there yesterday.

> I've **seen** it before.

> She **told** me everything.

> I **thought** you knew.

> We've **made** a mistake.

> He's **gone** home.

> I've **forgotten** his name.

> She **gave** me a call.

> I **left** my phone at home.

当这些形式越来越熟，不规则动词就不会再表现成一张需要临时搜索的表。

它们会逐渐变成英语表达里的自动反应。
